## What is the Crimson Isle?

The **Crimson Isle** is a mid-to-late game zone featuring:
- **Faction system** (Mages vs. Barbarians)
- **Blaze Slayer** content
- **Dojo** combat challenges
- **Kuudra** co-op boss
- **Lava Fishing**
- Access to powerful Crimson gear and Nether Wart farming

<Info title="How to unlock">
Complete the **Blazing Fortress** quest chain in the Hub, then find the portal to the Crimson Isle. You'll need Combat Skill 15+ and basic combat gear to start.
</Info>

---

## Faction System

On arrival you must choose a faction — this is **permanent per profile**:

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Mages">

**Recommended for most players**

- Magic and Intelligence focus
- Better synergy with Kuudra content
- Access to Mage-specific NPCs and items
- Generally preferred because Kuudra rewards are faction-neutral

</Tab>
<Tab label="Barbarians">

**Recommended for Berserk/melee focused players**

- Strength and melee focus
- Access to Barbarian-specific NPCs and items
- Some exclusive faction quests and gear

</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning title="Faction choice is permanent per profile">
You cannot freely switch factions. Choose based on your playstyle. Most players choose **Mages** for the Kuudra content synergy.
</Warning>

## Reputation System

Earning **Reputation** with your faction unlocks better items and Kuudra access.

| Source | Reputation Gained |
|--------|------------------|
| Completing quests | 100–500 |
| Killing Crimson Isle mobs | 1–5 per kill |
| Turning in faction items | Variable |
| Dojo completion | 50–200 |

**Reputation tiers** unlock:
- Faction-specific accessories
- Crimson Isle gear pieces
- Access to higher Kuudra tiers (via reputation gates)

---

## The Dojo

The Dojo is a series of combat challenges in the Crimson Isle. Complete them for **Reputation** and **SkyBlock XP**.

Challenges test:
- Speed (move through obstacles quickly)
- Damage (reach a damage threshold)
- Survival (take hits without dying)

Most challenges are completable with mid-game gear. Some endgame challenges require strong setups.

## Blaze Slayer

The **Inferno Demonlord** is the Blaze Slayer boss. Unique fire-based mechanics — fire resistance is essential.

<TableCard title="Blaze Slayer Tiers">

| Tier | Slayer XP | Key Drop |
|------|----------|---------|
| T1 | 5 | Ember Rod |
| T2 | 25 | Magma Lord armor pieces |
| T3 | 100 | Lava Flame pet shards |
| T4 | 400 | Fiery Kuudra set pieces, rare drops |

</TableCard>

<Tip title="Fire resistance is essential">
The Blaze boss deals heavy fire damage. Equip **Fire Protection IV** on armor and use a **Fire Resistance Potion** to survive comfortably.
</Tip>

The boss launches **fireballs** that deal massive damage if they connect. Stand at medium range and sidestep them.

## Lava Fishing

Lava Fishing in the Crimson Isle is one of the best mid-game fishing spots.

| Creature | Notable Drop |
|---------|-------------|
| Lava Flame | Uncommon pet |
| Fire Eel | Fire Eel pet (sells well) |
| Moogma | Rare drops |
| Taurus | Rare drops |
| Thunder | Very rare storm creature |

You **must use a lava-compatible rod** — the cheapest being the **Hellfire Rod** from the Crimson Isle merchant.

---

## Nether Wart Farming

The Crimson Isle is the only place to grow **Nether Wart**, one of the highest-value crops in the game.

- Farm in the Crimson Isle farming plots
- Requires Crimson Isle access and basic farming gear
- One of the best endgame pest-farming crops
