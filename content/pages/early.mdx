## Where to Start

When you create a new SkyBlock profile, your goal is simple: **generate coins, level skills, and buy better gear**. Don't try to do everything at once — pick one lane and push it.

<Info title="First thing to do">
Open your **SkyBlock Menu** (click the icon in your hotbar), then go to **Your Skills**. Every XP you earn levels skills that give permanent stat bonuses — this is your foundation.
</Info>

<Tip title="Fairy Souls are free stats">
There are **227 Fairy Souls** hidden across all SkyBlock islands. Trading pairs to Tia the Fairy gives permanent HP, Defense, Strength, and Speed. They cost nothing — just time to collect.
</Tip>

## Early Game Gear Targets

<TableCard title="Gear Progression by Slot" description="Don't rush expensive gear — a solid Hardened Diamond set with good enchants outperforms expensive gear you can't effectively use.">

| Slot | Budget Target | Upgrade To |
|------|--------------|------------|
| Weapon | Aspect of the End (AotE) | Raider Axe → Giant's Sword |
| Helmet | Hardened Diamond | Strong Dragon |
| Chestplate | Hardened Diamond | Strong Dragon |
| Leggings | Hardened Diamond | Strong Dragon |
| Boots | Hardened Diamond | Tarantula Boots (Spider Slayer) |
| Pet | Lvl 1 any combat pet | Lvl 100 Tiger or Enderman |

</TableCard>

<Warning title="Don't rush expensive gear">
Many early players spend their first 5M on gear they can't use effectively. Focus on Skill Average 20+ first — it makes every piece of gear more effective due to stat scaling.
</Warning>

## How to Get Your First Coins

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Farming">

**Easiest, no requirements**

- Buy seeds from the Farm Merchant in the Hub
- Plant and harvest on your Private Island
- Sell to the Bazaar (check prices first) or NPC traders
- **Aim for**: 100k–300k coins/hr to start

Once you unlock the Garden, farming XP and coins triple — prioritize the Garden unlock quest.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Mining">

**Good XP + coins**

- Head to the Gold Mine (portal in Hub)
- Mine Coal, Iron, Gold and sell on Bazaar
- Upgrade to Deep Caverns when possible
- **Aim for**: 100k–500k coins/hr

Eventually leads to Dwarven Mines and HotM — one of the best endgame lanes.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Combat">

**Spider's Den / Graveyard**

- Kill spiders in Spider's Den and sell String/Tarantula Web
- Hub Graveyard Zombies for early Combat XP
- Requires basic combat gear first
- **Aim for**: 200k–500k coins/hr

Combat XP also builds toward Dungeons and Slayers — high long-term value.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## SkyBlock Levels

SkyBlock Levels (shown as ✪ next to your name) are earned by completing milestones, quests, and achievements. Higher SB levels unlock:
- More **minion slots**
- Access to some locked areas
- Cosmetic perks

**First goal**: Reach **SkyBlock Level 10** — this unlocks full minion functionality.

---

## Minions — Your Passive Income

Minions automate resource generation on your Private Island, working even while you're offline.

| Minion | Approximate passive/hr | Setup Cost |
|--------|----------------------|------------|
| T1 Snow/Clay | ~50k | Free to start |
| T7 Wheat/Sugar Cane | ~200k | ~100k |
| T11 Farming | ~500k+ | ~1M+ |

<Info title="Unlock more minion slots">
You start with **5 slots**. Each unique minion type crafted (across all your profiles) increases your global slot cap. Craft cheap T1 minions of different types early to quickly expand to 9–11 slots.
</Info>

## Skills to Prioritize

| Skill | Why | How |
|-------|-----|-----|

| Farming | Huge Health bonus at high levels | Garden or hub farming |

## Common Early Game Mistakes

<Checklist title="Avoid these mistakes">
- Buying gear before checking requirements — always verify first
- Ignoring Enchanting — cheap enchants can double your damage
- Hoarding everything — sell what you don't need for faster upgrades
- Selling to NPCs without checking Bazaar first — Bazaar is almost always better
- Skipping Fairy Souls — free stats, always worth collecting
- Not setting up minions early — passive income compounds over time
</Checklist>
