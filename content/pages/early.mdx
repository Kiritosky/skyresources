## Where to Start

When you create a new SkyBlock profile, your goal is simple: **generate coins, level skills, and buy better gear**. Don't try to do everything at once — pick one lane and push it.

<Info title="First thing to do">
Open your **SkyBlock Menu** (click the icon in your hotbar), then go to **Your Skills**. Every XP you earn levels skills that give permanent stat bonuses — this is your foundation.
</Info>

---

## Essential First Steps

1. **Complete the tutorial quests** — they give free gear, coins, and SkyBlock XP.
2. **Buy a Rookie Hoe** or basic sword from the Hub NPCs to start farming/fighting.
3. **Talk to every NPC** in the Hub to unlock new areas, quests, and shops.
4. **Start grinding one skill** — Combat, Farming, or Mining. All give useful early bonuses.
5. **Collect Fairy Souls** — they permanently boost your stats. Grab as many as you can.

<Tip title="Fairy Souls are free stats">
There are 227 Fairy Souls hidden across all SkyBlock islands. Trading pairs of them to Tia the Fairy gives permanent HP, Defense, Strength, and Speed. Start collecting early — it costs nothing.
</Tip>

## How to Get Your First Coins

### Method 1 — Farming (easiest)
- Buy seeds from the Farm Merchant in the Hub
- Plant and harvest on your Private Island
- Sell to the Bazaar or NPC traders
- **Aim for**: 100k–300k coins/hr to start

### Method 2 — Mining
- Head to the Gold Mine (portal in Hub)
- Mine Coal, Iron, Gold, and sell on Bazaar
- Upgrade to Deep Caverns when possible
- **Aim for**: 100k–500k coins/hr

### Method 3 — Combat (Spider's Den / Crimson Isle)
- Kill spiders and sell String/Tarantula Web
- Requires basic combat gear first
- **Aim for**: 200k–500k coins/hr

## SkyBlock Levels (SB XP)

SkyBlock Levels (shown as ✪ next to your name) are earned by completing milestones, quests, and achievements. Higher SB levels unlock:
- Better minion slots
- Access to some areas
- Cosmetic perks

**Your first goal**: Reach **SkyBlock Level 10** — this unlocks full minion functionality.

---

## Minions — Your Passive Income

Minions are small automated workers on your Private Island. They generate resources while you're offline.

| Minion Tier | Approximate passive/hr | Setup cost |
|-------------|----------------------|------------|
| T1 Snow/Clay | ~50k | Free to start |
| T7 Wheat/Sugar Cane | ~200k | ~100k |
| T11 Farming | ~500k+ | ~1M+ |

<Info title="Unlock more minion slots">
You start with **5 minion slots**. Each unique minion crafted (across all profiles) increases your global slot cap. Craft cheap T1 minions of different types to quickly unlock more slots.
</Info>

## Common Early Game Mistakes

- **Buying gear you can't use** — check requirements before spending.
- **Ignoring Enchanting** — even cheap enchants double your damage output.
- **Hoarding everything** — sell what you don't need; liquid coins let you upgrade faster.
- **Not using the Bazaar** — it's almost always better than selling to NPCs (check first).
- **Skipping Fairy Souls** — free stats, always worth collecting.
