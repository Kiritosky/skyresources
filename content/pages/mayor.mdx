## How Elections Work

A new mayor is elected every **5 SkyBlock days** (roughly 5 real-world days). During the last day of the previous term a **voting period** opens where players cast SkyBlock Votes.

- The current mayor is visible in your SkyBlock Menu (press **F**).
- Track upcoming elections at [SkyBlock.tools/election](https://skyblock.tools/election).
- Each mayor has **3 perks** — but only some are active each term. The active perks rotate.

<Info title="Special mayors">
**Scorpius**, **Derpy**, and **Jerry** are special candidates that appear occasionally. They almost always win due to unusually powerful perks. Check the election tracker — when they appear, prep early.
</Info>

---

## Minister System

The **runner-up** in each election becomes the **Minister** — they get **1 perk** active during the term (their choice). This means two mayors are active simultaneously.

<Details title="Why does the minister matter?">
The minister's single active perk can significantly change what content is buffed. For example, if Diana wins but Cole is minister with the Mining Fortune perk, that term is buffed for both hunting and mining. Always check who the minister is, not just who won.
</Details>

## Regular Mayors

These 8 mayors appear on the standard election ballot:

<TableCard title="Regular Mayors" description="These candidates appear every election cycle on the standard ballot.">

| Mayor | Theme | Key Perks |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Aatrox | Combat | +100 Slayer XP, double Slayer drops, War bonus |
| Cole | Mining | +100 Mining Fortune, extra Powder, Comet buff |
| Diana | Hunt | Mythological Ritual (burrows + mythological mobs), Pelt trading |
| Diaz | Economy | Bonus auction slots, merchant discounts |
| Foxy | Fun | +25 Speed, Lucky Wheel, bonus event coins |
| Finnegan | Farming | Crop milestone XP boost, farming item buffs |
| Marina | Fishing | +Fishing XP, +Sea Creature Chance, +Luck of the Sea |
| Paul | Sports | PvP damage boost, mobility buffs |

</TableCard>

<Warning title="Perks rotate each term">
The same mayor can be stronger or weaker depending on which 3 perks they bring to a given election. Always check the active perks when an election opens, not just who the mayor is.
</Warning>

## Special Mayors

These candidates appear rarely and replace a slot on the ballot when they do. They nearly always win.

<TableCard title="Special Mayors" description="Only appear occasionally — prep well in advance when they show up.">

| Mayor | When Active | Key Perks |
|-------|------------|----------|
| Scorpius | Occasionally | Bribe (+free SkyBlock XP/day), +100 SkyBlock XP |
| Derpy | Occasionally | Triple stats (Health, Defense, Strength) and mob health × 3 |
| Jerry | Occasionally | 3 random perks taken from any other mayor's pool |

</TableCard>

## Mayor-by-Mayor Guide

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Diana">

**Best for: Mythological mob farming**

Diana's **Mythological Ritual** perk spawns burrows across SkyBlock islands. Dig them up with a shovel to find mythological mobs that drop:

- **Griffin Feathers** (very valuable)
- **Chimera** accessory ingredients
- **SkyBlock XP drops**

**Prep checklist:**

<Checklist title="Diana prep">
- Get a Griffin pet (any rarity works to start)
- Buy or craft a Shovel
- Stock up on Inquisitor-hunting gear if you want rare drops
- Check COFL for mythological mob drop prices before the term starts
</Checklist>

</Tab>
<Tab label="Cole">

**Best for: Mining and Powder farming**

Cole's perks include:
- **+100 Mining Fortune** — massive drop multiplier
- Bonus Powder from ores
- Forge time reductions via **Comet**

Best time to grind gemstones, push HotM levels, and farm for Divan's Armor pieces.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Marina">

**Best for: Fishing**

Marina gives +Sea Creature Chance and a Fishing XP multiplier. Best time to:
- Grind Trophy Fish for Shredder materials
- Level the Fishing skill faster
- Catch rare sea creatures for Bestiary

</Tab>
<Tab label="Scorpius">

**Best for: SkyBlock XP farming**

Scorpius's **Bribe** perk gives free SkyBlock XP every day — often thousands of XP per day passively. Very strong for unlocking higher SB Levels. Usually wins elections by a landslide when present.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Derpy">

**Best for: Slayer and fast content**

Derpy (special mayor) triples all player stats and all mob stats simultaneously:
- Mobs have 3× HP but also drop 3× coins
- Slayer bosses die faster due to your 3× stats
- Combat XP and coins both spike

Chaos ensues but the profit is real.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Jerry">

**Best for: Wildcard**

Jerry's perks are randomly selected from other mayors' pools. Could be powerful (e.g. 3 mining perks) or completely useless. Check the active perks the moment Jerry is elected before planning.

</Tab>
</Tabs>
