## How Elections Work

A mayor is elected every **5 SkyBlock days** (~5 real-world days). During the last day of a mayor's term, a **voting period** opens where players cast SkyBlock Votes for the next mayor.

- Mayors create **time-limited buff windows** for specific activities.
- The current mayor is visible in your SkyBlock Menu (F key).
- Track upcoming elections at [SkyBlock.tools/election](https://skyblock.tools/election).

<Info title="Special mayors">
**Scorpius**, **Derpy**, and **Jerry** are special candidates that appear occasionally instead of the regular slate. They almost always win due to unique or powerful perks. Prep for them early when they appear.
</Info>

</Warning>

## Mayor-by-Mayor Guide

### Diana — Best for: Mythological mob farming
Diana's **Mythological Ritual** perk causes burrows to spawn across SkyBlock islands, which can be dug up to find mythological mobs. These drop:
- **Griffin Feathers** (very valuable)
- **Chimera Talisman** ingredients
- **SkyBlock XP drops**

This is one of the best events for rare accessory farming. Prep by having a **Griffin pet** and a **Shovel** ready.

---

### Cole — Best for: Mining
Cole's **Comet** and **Forge the Galaxy** perks give:
- +100 Mining Fortune (huge drop multiplier)
- Bonus Powder from ores
- Forge time reductions

Best time to grind gemstones and push HotM levels.

---

### Marina — Best for: Fishing
Marina gives +Sea Creature Chance and +Fishing XP multiplier. Best time to:
- Grind Trophy Fish for Shredder materials
- Level Fishing skill faster
- Catch rare sea creatures

---

### Scorpius — Best for: SkyBlock XP farming
Scorpius's **Bribe** perk gives free SkyBlock XP every day. Very strong for unlocking higher SB Levels. Usually wins elections by a landslide.

---

### Derpy — The Event Mayor
Derpy (special/rare mayor) causes:
- All stats × 3 (health, defense, strength)
- Mob health × 3
- Coins from mobs × 3
- Chaos overall

Best for: fast Slayer completion (bosses die fast, coins increased).

---

### Jerry — Wildcard
Jerry's perks are randomly selected from other mayors. Could be amazing or useless depending on what's rolled. Check the perk list when Jerry is elected before planning.

## Preparing for a Mayor

1. **Check who's upcoming** — [SkyBlock.tools/election](https://skyblock.tools/election) shows candidates 1–2 terms in advance.
2. **Stock items cheaply before** — prices rise when the mayor term starts.
3. **Plan your grind** — know exactly what you'll be doing during the mayor's term.
4. **Sell after** — prices often normalize or drop after a term ends.

<Tip title="Don't over-prep">
The safest prep is stocking items you'd use anyway. Avoid buying 50M worth of items for a "buff window" you might not use fully. Moderate, targeted prep beats gambling on market movements.
</Tip>
