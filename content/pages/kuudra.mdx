## What is Kuudra?

**Kuudra** is a large co-op boss in the Crimson Isle. It's the main endgame content alternative to Dungeons. Unlike Dungeons, Kuudra:
- Requires **2–4 players** (can technically solo with extreme gear)
- Takes place in a special Kuudra instance
- Drops **Crimson Armor** pieces and **Attribute shards**
- Rewards **Kuudra Tokens** and **Crimson Essence**

<Info title="Kuudra access">
Access Kuudra by talking to the NPC in the **Crimson Isle** dock area. You need to have progressed through the Crimson Isle faction quests first.
</Info>

## Kuudra Tiers

| Tier | Difficulty | Entry Req. | Key Drops |
|------|-----------|------------|----------|
| T1 Basic | Very Easy | None | Basic Crimson pieces, tokens |
| T2 Hot | Easy | Basic combat gear | Better Crimson pieces |
| T3 Burning | Moderate | Mid gear (~400 MP) | Good Crimson pieces |
| T4 Fiery | Hard | Strong gear (600+ MP) | Best non-Infernal items |
| T5 Infernal | Extreme | Optimized team (700+ MP) | Infernal attributes, top rewards |

<Warning title="T5 is extremely gear-dependent">
T5 Infernal requires a well-coordinated party with near-endgame gear. Attempting T5 without proper setup will result in runs that fail or go very slowly. Master T4 first.
</Warning>

## The Attribute System

Kuudra drops **Crimson Armor** pieces with random **Attributes**. Attributes give powerful bonuses, and you can **combine** matching attributes to upgrade their tier (Tier 1 → Tier 5).

| Attribute | Effect | Best For |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Mana Pool | +Mana per Mana Regen tick | Mage |
| Lifeline | Heal on ability use | Healer/Tank |
| Dominance | +Crit Damage scaling | Archer |
| Blazing Fortune | +Crimson Isle mob luck | Blaze Slayer |
| Veteran | +Damage based on mob kills | Combat |
| Magic Find | +Bonus drops | Farming/Combat |
| Speed | +Speed | Movement |
| Fortitude | +Defense | Tank |

### Combining Attributes
- 2× Tier 1 → 1× Tier 2 (costs a Kuudra piece of each)
- 2× Tier 2 → 1× Tier 3
- Up to Tier 5 (maximum)

<Tip title="Attribute matching is key">
When combining, both pieces must have the **same attribute** in the same slot. Use COFL's attribute tool to find matching pieces on the Auction House. A good Tier 5 attribute can be worth hundreds of millions.
</Tip>

## Party Roles

A typical Kuudra party has 4 roles:

| Role | Job | Gear Focus |
|------|-----|-----------|
| Supplier | Collect materials during build phase | Speed, survivability |
| Builder | Construct the fuel cells | Speed, survivability |
| Damage | Deal damage to Kuudra | Max damage output |
| Tank/Support | Survive hits, help team | Defense, health |

In 2-player runs, two players handle supply/build and two handle DPS. Flexible depending on party composition.

---

## Kuudra Fight Phases

### Phase 1 — Supply & Build
- Collect **Supplies** from crates across the arena
- Use supplies to build **Fuel Cells** at the designated spots
- Complete 4 cells to progress

### Phase 2 — DPS Kuudra
- Kuudra's weak spot is exposed
- All players deal damage to Kuudra
- Avoid lava and fireballs
- Boss has HP phases — watch for enrage transitions

<Warning title="Kuudra can instakill">
Kuudra's fireball and ground slam can one-shot players with low defense. Stay mobile, avoid standing in lava, and communicate with your party.
</Warning>

## Kuudra Token Shop

Kuudra Tokens (earned each run) can be spent in the Kuudra Shop on:
- Crimson Essence (for Kuudra gear upgrades)
- Specific attribute upgrade materials
- Cosmetics

## Getting Started

1. **Unlock the Crimson Isle** and reach T3 reputation threshold.
2. **Build basic gear**: ~400 MP and decent dungeon/slayer gear for T1–T2.
3. **Learn the mechanic**: run T1–T2 to understand Supply/Build phases.
4. **Push to T4**: this is where coins/hr becomes worthwhile.
5. **Upgrade Crimson pieces**: use Attribute combining for endgame value.
