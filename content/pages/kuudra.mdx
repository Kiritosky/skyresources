## What is Kuudra?

**Kuudra** is a large co-op boss in the Crimson Isle — the main endgame content alternative to Dungeons. Unlike Dungeons, Kuudra:
- Requires **2–4 players** (can technically solo with extreme gear)
- Drops **Crimson Armor** pieces with random **Attributes**
- Rewards **Kuudra Tokens** and **Crimson Essence**
- Has a unique **Supply & Build** mechanic in Phase 1

<Info title="Kuudra access">
Access Kuudra via the NPC at the **Crimson Isle** dock area. You need to have progressed through the Crimson Isle faction quests and reached the required reputation threshold first.
</Info>

<Warning title="T5 is extremely gear-dependent">

</Warning>

## The Attribute System

Kuudra drops **Crimson Armor** with random **Attributes**. Matching attributes can be **combined** to upgrade their tier (T1 → T5), making them exponentially more powerful.

<TableCard title="Notable Attributes">

| Attribute | Effect | Best For |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Mana Pool | +Mana per Mana Regen tick | Mage |
| Lifeline | Heal on ability use | Healer/Tank |
| Dominance | +Crit Damage scaling | Archer |
| Blazing Fortune | +Crimson Isle mob luck | Blaze Slayer |
| Veteran | +Damage based on mob kills | Combat |
| Magic Find | +Bonus drops | Farming/Combat |
| Speed | +Speed | Movement |
| Fortitude | +Defense | Tank |

</TableCard>

### Combining Attributes

To upgrade an attribute tier, combine two identical attribute pieces:
- 2× Tier 1 → 1× Tier 2
- 2× Tier 2 → 1× Tier 3
- Continues up to **Tier 5** (maximum)

<Tip title="Attribute matching with COFL">
Both pieces must have the **same attribute** in the same slot. Use COFL's attribute search tool to find matching pieces on the Auction House. A well-rolled Tier 5 attribute can be worth hundreds of millions.
</Tip>

## Party Roles

<TableCard title="Kuudra Party Roles">

| Role | Job | Gear Focus |
|------|-----|-----------|
| Supplier | Collect materials from crates across the arena | Speed, survivability |
| Builder | Construct the Fuel Cells | Speed, survivability |
| Damage | Deal damage to Kuudra | Max damage output |
| Tank/Support | Survive hits, help party | Defense, health |

</TableCard>

In 2-player runs, both players share supply/build and DPS duties. Flexible based on composition.
