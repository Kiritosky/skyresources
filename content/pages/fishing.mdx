## Fishing Overview

Fishing in SkyBlock is much deeper than vanilla Minecraft. You catch **Sea Creatures** instead of just fish, and different locations produce different creatures and items. Fishing also contributes to your **Fishing Skill XP** and unlocks powerful gear.

<Info title="Sea Creature Chance">
Your **Sea Creature Chance (SCC)** stat determines how often you catch a sea creature vs. a regular fish. Higher SCC = more sea creature loot. Aim for at least 30% SCC before grinding sea creature drops.
</Info>

## Rod Progression

| Rod | Where to get | Notes |
|-----|-------------|-------|
| Rod of Legends | Fisherman merchant or AH | Starting rod |
| Auger Rod | Crafted | First upgrade, adds Sea Creature Chance |
| Ammonite's Pick | Crafted | Best early fishing rod |
| Shredder | Crafted (Trophy Fish) | Best for catching sea creatures |
| Rod of the Sea | Crafted | Endgame AoE rod |
| Shredder (AOTS build) | Crafted | Best for coins/hr in lava fishing |

<Tip title="Shredder First">
The **Shredder** is one of the first "real" rods you should aim for. It pulls multiple fish at once when enchanted with Spiked Hook, dramatically increasing loot/hr.
</Tip>

## Fishing Zones

### Ocean / Hub
Best for early game. Catch basic fish + sea creatures like Squid, Sea Walker, Sea Guardian.

### Frozen Ocean (Jerry's Workshop — Winter only)
Best **early Fishing XP**. During Jerry's Workshop events the pond has increased sea creature spawns.

### Spooky Fishing (Halloween)
Seasonal. Catch Werewolves, Water Hydras, Sea Witches. Good for **Wizard Hats** and loot bags.

### Barn / Mushroom Desert
Used mainly for **Trophy Fishing**. Unique creatures like the Catfish, Nurse Shark, and Blue Whale. Barn fishing drops **Trophy Fish** that can be traded for high-value items.

### Lava Fishing (Crimson Isle)
Best fishing XP and coins/hr in mid-to-late game. Requires a lava rod (e.g., Hellfire Rod). Catch **Lava Flame**, **Fire Eel**, and **Moogma** for profitable drops.

<Warning title="Lava Rods Required">
Regular water rods do **not** work in lava. You need a lava-specific rod. The cheapest good option is the **Hellfire Rod** from the Crimson Isle merchant.
</Warning>

## Sea Creature Chance (SCC)

SCC is your most important fishing stat. Sources:

| Source | SCC Bonus |
|--------|-----------|
| Fishing Level (each level) | +0.2% per level |
| Shark Scale Boots | +5% |
| Rod enchant: Expertise | Up to +10% |
| Rod enchant: Blessing | +12.5% |
| Fishing Helmet (Diver set) | Small bonus |
| Dolphin/Whale pet | Passive bonus per level |
| Sonar Device (talisman) | +5% |
| Spooky Festival buff | +25% temporary |

## Trophy Fishing

Trophy Fishing is a special fishing mechanic in the **Barn** area. You catch rare **Trophy Fish** that can be traded to Odger in exchange for:
- **Shredder rod** materials
- **Bronze/Silver/Gold trophies** (for cosmetics and SkyBlock XP)
- **Fishing-specific accessories**

Trophy Fish tiers: Bronze → Silver → Gold. Higher tiers are rarer and worth more.

<Info title="Rodent Fishing">
The **Shredder** rod is crafted from Trophy Fish materials. It is one of the best fishing rods in the game for pulling in multiple catches simultaneously.
</Info>

## Fishing Pets

| Pet | Best for | Notes |
|-----|----------|-------|
| Dolphin | Early–mid XP | Passive Fishing XP multiplier |
| Whale | Mid–late XP + drops | +Fishing Fortune per rarity level |
| Squid | Magic Find build | Drops rare items more often |
| Flying Fish | Sea creature coins | Boosts sea creature drops |
| Ocelot | Foraging (not fishing) | Don't confuse with fishing pets |

<Tip title="Dolphin → Whale Transition">
Use a **Legendary Dolphin** until you can afford a **Legendary Whale**. The Whale's Fishing Fortune bonus is enormous and directly affects your drop rates.
</Tip>

## Getting Started: Fishing Route

1. **Buy** a Rod of Legends from the Fisherman shop
2. **Enchant** it with Blessing 1 and Expertise 1
3. **Fish** at the Hub lake to level Fishing to 20
4. **Get** a Dolphin pet (common/uncommon is fine early)
5. **Upgrade** to Auger Rod → Ammonite's Pick for better SCC
6. **Move** to Lava Fishing in Crimson Isle at Fishing 30+
7. **Grind** Trophy Fish in the Barn for Shredder materials
