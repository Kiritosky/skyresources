## Fishing Overview

Fishing in SkyBlock is much deeper than vanilla Minecraft. You catch **Sea Creatures** instead of just fish, and different locations produce different creatures and loot. Fishing also gives **Fishing Skill XP** and unlocks powerful gear.

<Info title="Sea Creature Chance">
Your **Sea Creature Chance (SCC)** determines how often you catch a sea creature vs. a regular fish. Higher SCC = more sea creature loot. Aim for at least 30% SCC before grinding sea creature drops.
</Info>

## Fishing Zones

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Ocean / Hub">

**Best for: Early game, Fishing Skill 1–20**

Basic fish and sea creatures: Squid, Sea Walker, Sea Guardian. Good starting zone before you unlock better spots.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Frozen Ocean">

**Best for: Early Fishing XP (winter event)**

During Jerry's Workshop (Winter events), the pond has increased sea creature spawns. Excellent early XP.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Barn (Trophy Fishing)">

**Best for: Trophy Fish farming**

Unique creatures: Catfish, Nurse Shark, Blue Whale. Drops **Trophy Fish** that are traded to Odger for Shredder materials and accessories.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Lava Fishing (Crimson Isle)">

**Best for: Mid-late coins/hr and XP**

Requires a lava-specific rod. Catch **Lava Flame**, **Fire Eel**, and **Moogma** for high-value drops.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning title="Lava Rods Required">
Regular water rods do **not** work in lava. You need a lava-specific rod. The cheapest good option is the **Hellfire Rod** from the Crimson Isle merchant.
</Warning>

## Trophy Fishing

Trophy Fishing is a special mechanic in the **Barn** area. You catch rare **Trophy Fish** in Bronze → Silver → Gold tiers. Higher tiers are rarer and worth more.

Trade them to **Odger** in the Barn for:
- **Shredder rod** crafting materials
- **Fishing-specific accessories**
- Bronze/Silver/Gold trophies (cosmetics and SkyBlock XP)

<Info title="Fish in Barn, not the ocean, for Trophy Fish">
Trophy Fish only spawn in the Barn area. The correct body of water is the lake near the Barn island — not regular ocean fishing spots.
</Info>

## Fishing Pets

| Pet | Best For | Notes |
|-----|----------|-------|
| Dolphin | Early–mid Fishing XP | Passive XP multiplier |
| Whale | Mid–late drops + XP | +Fishing Fortune per rarity |
| Squid | Magic Find builds | Rare items more often |
| Flying Fish | Sea creature coins | Boosts sea creature drops |

<Tip title="Dolphin → Whale transition">
Use a **Legendary Dolphin** until you can afford a **Legendary Whale**. The Whale's Fishing Fortune bonus directly multiplies your drop rates.
</Tip>
