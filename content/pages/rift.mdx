## What is the Rift?

The **Rift** is a separate dimension in SkyBlock with unique mechanics, currency, and progression. It's themed around a corrupted, time-warped world. Notable features:

- **Motes** — the Rift's currency (not coins — cannot be transferred to main profile)
- **Timecharms** — special items that unlock Rift zones
- **Enigma Souls** — collectibles that give permanent bonuses
- **Vampire Slayer** — Rift-exclusive slayer type
- **Mirrorverse** — a reflection dimension inside the Rift

<Warning title="Rift is isolated">
Most gear, coins, and items do **not** transfer to the Rift. You use a separate **Rift inventory**, and only specific items work in the Rift. Motes cannot be taken out of the Rift.
</Warning>

---

## How to Unlock the Rift

1. Reach a certain SkyBlock Level (check the Rift NPC in the Hub for current requirements).
2. Talk to **Elise** in the Hub to start the Rift unlock quest.
3. Complete the introductory quest chain.
4. Enter via the Rift portal in the Hub.

---

## Timecharms

Timecharms unlock new areas within the Rift. There are multiple timecharms, each opening a specific zone:

| Timecharm | Zone Unlocked | How to Get |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| Wyld Wynd | Wyld Wynd area | Quest chain |
| Mirrorverse | Mirror dimension | Progress quest |
| Vampiric | Vampiric Mine | Mid-progression |
| Living Cave | Underground cave zone | Quest |
| Lagoon | Lagoon area | Quest |

Collect all Timecharms to fully unlock the Rift.

## Motes — The Rift Currency

**Motes** are earned in the Rift and spent in the Rift. They buy:
- Rift gear upgrades
- Special Rift items
- Access to the Rift's Traveling Merchant

### Best Motes Farming Methods

| Method | Motes/hr | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|
| Montezuma's Abyss (mob farming) | High | Best density of motes drops |
| Vampire Slayer quests | Moderate | Doubles as slayer XP |
| Rift quests | Variable | One-time high rewards |
| Wyld Wynd mobs | Moderate | Easy access |

<Tip title="Motes from Vampiric area">
The **Vampiric Mine** area has dense vampire mobs that drop Motes consistently. This is also where you do Vampire Slayer quests, so you efficiently earn both at once.
</Tip>

## Enigma Souls

**Enigma Souls** are collectibles hidden throughout the Rift (similar to Fairy Souls but Rift-specific). Collecting them gives permanent bonuses to your Rift stats.

There are 42 Enigma Souls total. Finding all of them gives a substantial permanent bonus.

<Info title="Enigma Soul guide">
Search YouTube for "Hypixel SkyBlock all Enigma Soul locations" for a video guide — they're hidden in various nooks throughout all Rift zones.
</Info>

## Vampire Slayer

The **Vampire Slayer** is unique to the Rift. The boss is the **Riftstalker Bloodfiend**.

**How it works differently:**
- Uses **Motes** instead of coins for the quest fee
- Boss mechanic: Vampires regenerate health — use **Garlic** and **Wooden Stake** mechanics to counter regen
- The boss has phases tied to its health

| Tier | Motes Cost | Slayer XP | Key Drop |
|------|-----------|----------|---------|
| T1 | Low | 5 | Vampire Fang |
| T2 | Moderate | 25 | Vampire Fang, accessories |
| T3 | High | 100 | Rare drops |
| T4 | Very High | 400 | Best drops |

<Warning title="Vampire boss regen mechanic">
The Vampiric boss regenerates health rapidly unless you use the correct counter-items. Bring **Garlic** (from the Rift merchant) and a **Wooden Stake** to properly combat the boss. Without these, T3/T4 is extremely difficult.
</Warning>

## Mirrorverse

The **Mirrorverse** is a reflection of SkyBlock's main areas inside the Rift. It's used for:
- Special quests and events
- Unique mob farming
- Enigma Soul locations

---

## Getting Started in the Rift

1. Unlock the Rift via Elise's quest.
2. Collect the first **Timecharm** (Wyld Wynd) to open the first area.
3. Explore the Wyld Wynd and complete early quests for Motes.
4. Push toward the **Vampiric Mine** for Vampire Slayer access.
5. Collect **Enigma Souls** as you explore each area.
6. Gradually unlock all Timecharm zones.
