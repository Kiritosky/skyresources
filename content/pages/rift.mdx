## What is the Rift?

The **Rift** is a separate dimension in SkyBlock with its own inventory, currency, and progression systems — themed around a corrupted, time-warped world.

<StatRow>
  <Stat label="Currency" value="Motes" note="Rift-exclusive — cannot leave the Rift" />
  <Stat label="Collectibles" value="42" note="Enigma Souls hidden across all Rift zones" />
  <Stat label="Unique Slayer" value="Vampire" note="Only slayer type found in the Rift" />
  <Stat label="Zone Unlocks" value="Timecharms" note="Each charm opens a new Rift area" />
</StatRow>

<Warning title="Rift is isolated">
Most gear, coins, and items do **not** transfer to the Rift. You use a separate **Rift inventory**, and only specific items work inside. Motes cannot be taken out of the Rift.
</Warning>

## Timecharms

Timecharms unlock new areas within the Rift. Collect all of them to fully explore.

<TableCard title="Timecharm Zones">

| Timecharm | Zone Unlocked | How to Get |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| Wyld Wynd | Wyld Wynd area | Given on first entry |
| Mirrorverse | Mirror dimension | Progress through quests |

| Living Cave | Underground cave zone | Quest chain |

## Motes — The Rift Currency

**Motes** are earned in the Rift and spent exclusively in the Rift. They buy gear upgrades, special items, and Traveling Merchant stock.

### Best Motes Farming Methods

| Method | Motes/hr | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|
| Montezuma's Abyss (mob farming) | High | Best mob density for Motes drops |
| Vampire Slayer quests | Moderate | Doubles as Slayer XP |
| Wyld Wynd mobs | Moderate | Easy access from spawn |
| Rift quests | Variable | One-time high rewards |

<Tip title="Vampiric Mine doubles up">
The Vampiric Mine has dense vampire mobs that drop Motes consistently. Doing Vampire Slayer here earns both Motes and Slayer XP simultaneously.
</Tip>

## Enigma Souls

**Enigma Souls** are collectibles hidden throughout the Rift — similar to Fairy Souls but Rift-specific. There are **42 total**. Collecting them gives permanent Rift stat bonuses.

<Info title="Finding Enigma Souls">
Search YouTube for "Hypixel SkyBlock all Enigma Soul locations" for a video guide. They're hidden in nooks across all Rift zones — some are tricky to find without a guide.
</Info>

## Vampire Slayer

The **Riftstalker Bloodfiend** is the Vampire Slayer boss — unique to the Rift.

**How it differs from other slayers:**
- Uses **Motes** instead of coins for quest fees
- The boss **regenerates health rapidly** — you must use **Garlic** and **Wooden Stake** mechanics to counter it
- Has health-based phase transitions

<TableCard title="Vampire Slayer Tiers">

| Tier | Motes Cost | Slayer XP | Key Drop |
|------|-----------|----------|---------|
| T1 | Low | 5 | Vampire Fang |
| T2 | Moderate | 25 | Vampire Fang, accessories |
| T3 | High | 100 | Rare drops |
| T4 | Very High | 400 | Best drops, rare accessories |

</TableCard>

<Warning title="Vampire boss regen mechanic">
Without the correct counter-items, the boss heals faster than you can damage it. Bring **Garlic** (from the Rift merchant) and a **Wooden Stake**. T3/T4 is nearly impossible without these.
</Warning>

## Mirrorverse

The **Mirrorverse** is a reflection of SkyBlock's main areas inside the Rift. Used for:
- Special quests and events
- Unique mob farming (specific Bestiary entries)
- Hidden Enigma Soul locations

## Getting Started Checklist

<Checklist title="Rift progression checklist">
- Meet SkyBlock Level requirement and talk to Elise in the Hub
- Complete introductory quest chain to unlock the Rift portal
- Collect the Wyld Wynd Timecharm (given at start)
- Explore the Wyld Wynd and complete early Motes quests
- Buy Garlic and Wooden Stake from the Rift merchant
- Push toward the Vampiric Mine for Vampire Slayer
- Collect Enigma Souls as you explore each zone
- Unlock all Timecharm zones to fully explore the Rift
</Checklist>
