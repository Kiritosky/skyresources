## How Enchanting Works

Enchanting in SkyBlock works similarly to vanilla but with dozens of custom enchantments. You apply them via:
1. **Enchanting Table** — enchant items directly using levels
2. **Anvil** — combine enchantment books onto items
3. **Experimentation Table** — minigames that give XP and books as rewards

<Info title="Enchanting Skill XP">
Every enchantment applied, book combined, or Experimentation Table run gives **Enchanting XP**. Higher Enchanting gives more Intelligence (key for Mage builds) and better Experimentation Table rewards.
</Info>

---

## Experimentation Table

The **Experimentation Table** is the fastest source of Enchanting XP. It resets every **20 SkyBlock hours** (roughly every 20 real minutes). Access it from your Private Island.

| Tier | Enchanting Level Required | XP Reward |
|------|--------------------------|----------|
| Superpairs | 0 | Low |
| Chronomatron | 10 | Moderate |
| Ultrasequencer | 20 | High |

<Tip title="Do Experimentation Table every reset">
At Enchanting 60, the Experimentation Table gives millions of XP per reset. Running it takes 2–3 minutes and is the most time-efficient Enchanting XP method. Do it every single reset.
</Tip>

## Ultimate Enchantments

Ultimate enchants occupy a dedicated "ultimate" slot. **Only one ultimate per item.**

<TableCard title="Ultimate Enchants">

| Ultimate Enchant | Best For |
|------------------|---------|
| Ultimate Wise | Mana regen — Mage builds |
| Ultimate Chimera | Versatile DPS across classes |
| Ultimate Combo | Stacks on kill — mob grinding |
| Ultimate Soul Eater | Berserk / high kill rate |
| Ultimate Bank | XP farming |
| Ultimate Rend | Slayer / boss fights |
| Ultimate Jerry | Pure fun — not useful |

</TableCard>

<Warning title="Ultimate enchants are expensive">
Ultimate enchantment books can cost 10–100M coins. Don't buy them until you have a solid set of regular enchants first. All content up to M5 is completable without ultimates.
</Warning>

## Enchanting Book Combining

Books can be combined on the **Anvil** to reach higher levels:

- Level 1 + Level 1 = Level 2
- Level 2 + Level 2 = Level 3
- And so on up to max level

<Tip title="Buy books from Bazaar">
For most enchantments, buying the max-level book directly from the Bazaar is cheaper and faster than grinding for random books. Check Bazaar prices first — it's almost always the right call.
</Tip>

Books come from: Enchanting Table (random), dungeon chests, Bazaar (most reliable), and Experimentation Table rewards.

## FAQ

<Details title="Can I remove enchantments from items?">
Yes — use a **Book of Demascus** to strip a specific enchantment off an item into a book. These are sold on the Auction House. Useful when you accidentally apply the wrong enchant to an expensive piece.
</Details>

<Details title="What's the fastest way to level Enchanting?">
The **Experimentation Table** is by far the fastest method — run it every reset (every ~20 minutes). Supplement with combining books on the anvil and applying enchants to any gear you're upgrading.
</Details>
