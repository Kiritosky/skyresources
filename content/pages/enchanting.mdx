## How Enchanting Works

Enchanting in SkyBlock works similarly to vanilla but with dozens of custom enchantments. You apply enchantments via:
1. **Enchanting Table** — enchant items directly
2. **Anvil** — combine enchantment books onto items
3. **Experimentation Table** — minigames that give XP and books

<Info title="Enchanting Skill">
Every enchantment applied, book combined, or Experimentation Table run gives **Enchanting XP**. Higher Enchanting skill gives more Intelligence (a key Mage stat) and bonus XP from the Experimentation Table.
</Info>

---

## Experimentation Table

The **Experimentation Table** is the fastest source of Enchanting XP. It resets every **20 SkyBlock hours** (~20 real minutes).

Access it from your Private Island via the Experimentation Table block.

| Tier | Enchanting Level Required | XP Reward |
|------|--------------------------|----------|
| Superpairs | 0 | Low |
| Chronomatron | 10 | Moderate |
| Ultrasequencer | 20 | High |

<Tip title="Do Experimentation Table daily">
At Enchanting 60, the Experimentation Table gives millions of XP per reset. Run it every time it resets — it takes 2–3 minutes and is the most time-efficient XP source.
</Tip>

## Ultimate Enchantments

Ultimate enchants are tier-6 enchantments that go in a dedicated "ultimate" slot. Only one ultimate per item.

| Ultimate Enchant | Best For |
|------------------|---------|
| Ultimate Wise | Mana regen — Mage builds |
| Ultimate Jerry | Pure fun, not useful |
| Ultimate Chimera | Versatile DPS |
| Ultimate Combo | Stacks on kill — mob grinding |
| Ultimate Soul Eater | Berserk / high kill rate |
| Ultimate Bank | XP farming |
| Ultimate Rend | Slayer / boss fights |

<Warning title="Ultimate enchants are expensive">
Ultimate enchantment books can cost 10–100M coins. Don't buy them until you have a solid set of regular enchants first. Most content is completable without ultimates.
</Warning>

## Enchanting Book Combining

Enchantment books can be combined on the **Anvil** to reach higher levels. Each combine costs coins.

- Level 1 + Level 1 = Level 2
- Level 2 + Level 2 = Level 3
- And so on up to the max level for that enchant

Books are available from:
- Enchanting Table (random)
- Dungeon chests
- Bazaar (most common source for specific books)
- Experimentation Table rewards

<Tip title="Buy books from Bazaar">
For most enchantments, buying the max-level book directly from the Bazaar is cheaper and faster than grinding for random books. Check the Bazaar first.
</Tip>
