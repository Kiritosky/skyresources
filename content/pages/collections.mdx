## What Are Collections?

Collections track how much of each resource you've gathered across your profile. Reaching thresholds unlocks:
- Crafting **recipes** for tools, armor, and accessories
- **SkyBlock XP** milestones
- Unique **cosmetic rewards**
- Minion upgrade recipes

Resources count toward collections **even after you sell them** — so never worry about selling out.

<Info title="Collections are profile-specific">
Collections only count resources gathered on your current profile. If you switch profiles, you start fresh. Plan your profile choice carefully before grinding collections.
</Info>

<Tip title="Use minions to AFK collections">
Set up a T7+ minion for any collection you need but don't actively farm. Minions work 24/7 even offline — the most efficient way to push niche collections.
</Tip>

## Minions — Passive Income Setup

Minions generate resources on your Private Island. They're your primary passive income source.

### Minion Tier Scaling

| Tier | Speed (seconds/action) | Relative Income |
|------|----------------------|----------------|
| T1 | 26s | 1× |
| T5 | 13s | 2× |
| T7 | 9.5s | 2.7× |
| T9 | 7s | 3.7× |
| T11 | 5s | ~5× |

### Best Minions for Passive Coins

<TableCard title="Best Passive Income Minions" description="Approximate daily passive coins at T11 with fuel — prices vary.">

| Minion | Approx. coins/day | Notes |
|--------|------------------|-------|
| Snow / Clay | Low | Just for collection unlocks |
| Sugar Cane T11 | ~400k | Easy to set up |
| Wheat T11 | ~300k | Stable NPC price |
| Slime T11 | ~500k | Slimeball Bazaar price |
| Lapis T11 | ~600k | Enchanted Lapis prices |
| Alchemy T11 | ~800k | Needs Potion Mixer setup |
| Ghast T11 | ~1.2M | Best but most expensive to build |

</TableCard>

<Warning title="Minion coins scale with setup cost">
More expensive minions (T11, Slime, Ghast) give better returns but cost 10–50M to build. Start cheap and reinvest profits. Don't skip tiers.
</Warning>

## Minion Slot Unlocks

You start with **5 minion slots**. Crafting unique minion types (across all your profiles) increases your global cap.

| Unique minion types crafted | Total slots |
|----------------------------|-------------|
| 0 | 5 |
| 5 | 6 |
| 15 | 7 |
| 30 | 9 |
| 50 | 11 |
| 75 | 13 |
| 100 | 15 |

**Fastest path**: Craft T1 minions of many different cheap types (Snow, Clay, Flower, Cow, Chicken). Each unique type needs to be crafted once per account.

<Checklist title="Slot unlock targets">
- Craft 5 unique types to unlock slot 6 early
- Reach 15 unique types for 7 slots — very achievable early
- Aim for 30 unique types (9 slots) before investing heavily in expensive minions
</Checklist>
