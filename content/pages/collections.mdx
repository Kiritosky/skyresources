## What Are Collections?

Collections track how much of each resource you've gathered across your profile. As you reach thresholds, you unlock:
- Crafting **recipes** for tools, armor, and accessories
- **SkyBlock XP** milestones
- Unique **cosmetic rewards**
- Minion upgrade recipes

Collections are permanent — gathered resources count even after selling them.

<Info title="Collections are profile-specific">
Collections only count resources gathered on your current profile. If you switch profiles, you start fresh. Plan your profile choice carefully before grinding collections.
</Info>

## Priority Collections

These collections unlock items that are essential for progression. Push them early.

| Collection | Threshold | Why It Matters |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| Sugar Cane 9 | 50,000 | Unlocks Enchanted Sugar recipe |
| Wheat 9 | 50,000 | Unlocks Enchanted Bread and Bales |
| Pumpkin 9 | 50,000 | Farming gear prereq |
| Spider Eye 5 | 2,500 | Unlocks Tarantula-related recipes |
| Coal 4 | 5,000 | Enchanted Coal for minion fuel |
| Iron 4 | 5,000 | Enchanted Iron for early armor |
| Gold 4 | 5,000 | Enchanted Gold, furnace fuel |
| Lapis 9 | 50,000 | Lapis Armor (early combat set) |
| Redstone 9 | 50,000 | Redstone Minion, important recipes |
| Melon 7 | 25,000 | Enchanted Melon Blocks |

<Tip title="Use minions to AFK collections">
Set up a minion for any collection you need but don't actively farm. T7+ minions work 24/7 even when you're offline. This is the most efficient way to push niche collections.
</Tip>

## Minions — Passive Income Setup

Minions are the core passive income system. They generate resources on your Private Island.

### Minion Tier Scaling

| Tier | Speed (seconds/action) | Relative Income |
|------|----------------------|----------------|
| T1 | 26s | 1× |
| T5 | 13s | 2× |
| T7 | 9.5s | 2.7× |
| T9 | 7s | 3.7× |
| T11 | 5s | ~5× |

### Best Minions for Passive Coins

| Minion | Tier | Approx. coins/day | Notes |
|--------|------|------------------|-------|
| Snow / Clay | T1 | Low | Just for collection unlocks |
| Sugar Cane | T11 | ~400k | Easy to set up |
| Wheat | T11 | ~300k | Stable NPC price |
| Slime | T11 | ~500k | Slimeball Bazaar price |
| Lapis | T11 | ~600k | Enchanted Lapis prices |
| Alchemy | T11 | ~800k | Needs Potion Mixer setup |
| Ghast | T11 | ~1.2M | Good but costs more to craft |

<Warning title="Minion coins scale with setup cost">
More expensive minions (T11, Slime, Ghast) give better returns but cost 10–50M to build. Start cheap and reinvest profits. Don't skip tiers.
</Warning>

## Minion Upgrades & Fuel

Minions can be upgraded with:
- **Minion Expanders** — +1 storage capacity per expander (2 slots max per minion)
- **Super Compactors** — auto-compress drops into Enchanted items for more storage
- **Fuel** — greatly speeds up minion action rate:

| Fuel | Speed Boost | Duration |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Catalyst | +25% | Infinite |
| Hamster Wheel | +25% | Infinite |
| Foul Flesh | +20% | 3 days |
| Plasma Bucket | +15% | 1 day |
| Solar Panel | +25% (daytime only) | Infinite |
| Enchanted Lava Bucket | +25% | 12 days |

<Tip title="Best all-around fuel">
**Enchanted Lava Bucket** is the best long-duration fuel. **Hamster Wheel** (from Wolf Slayer) is the best infinite fuel. Use Hamster Wheel on your best minions and Enchanted Lava Bucket on the rest.
</Tip>

## Minion Slot Unlocks

You start with **5 minion slots**. Crafting unique minion types (across all profiles on your account) increases your global slot cap.

| Unique minions crafted | Total slots |
|----------------------|-------------|
| 0 | 5 |
| 5 | 6 |
| 15 | 7 |
| 30 | 9 |
| 50 | 11 |
| 75 | 13 |
| 100 | 15 |

**Fastest way to unlock slots**: Craft cheap T1 minions of many different types (Snow, Clay, Flower, Cow, etc.). Each unique type only needs to be crafted once.
