## Mining Overview

Mining in SkyBlock is a deep progression system built around the **Heart of the Mountain (HotM)** skill tree. Unlike vanilla Minecraft, you earn **Mining XP**, **Powder**, and special resources from ores that scale dramatically with your gear and HotM investment.

<Tip title="Unlock Dwarven Mines first">
To unlock HotM, talk to **Rhys** in the Dwarven Mines and give him 15× any enchanted ore (e.g. Enchanted Cobblestone). The Dwarven Mines are accessed via the portal in the Hub near the Gold Mine.
</Tip>

---

## Heart of the Mountain (HotM)

HotM is your mining skill tree — it has **10 tiers** and dozens of upgradable nodes. Leveling it up requires **Mithril Powder** (from Dwarven Mines) and **Gemstone Powder** (from Crystal Hollows).

### HotM Tier Milestones

| HotM Level | Key Unlock |
|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | Unlock HotM tree |
| 2 | Mining Speed II node |
| 3 | Quick Forge (faster crafting) |
| 4 | Crystal Hollows access |
| 5 | Efficient Miner node |
| 6 | Mining Speed Boost ability |
| 7 | Vein Seeker node |
| 8 | Glacite Tunnels access |
| 9 | Mineshaft access |
| 10 | Full tree, all nodes available |

<Info title="Token of the Mountain">
Each HotM level gives a **Token of the Mountain** which can be spent to unlock new HotM nodes (perks/abilities). Spend them wisely — focus on **Mining Speed** and **Mining Fortune** nodes first.
</Info>

## Powder Types

| Type | Where From | Used For |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Mithril Powder | Dwarven Mines, Glacite Tunnels | HotM upgrades |
| Gemstone Powder | Crystal Hollows | HotM upgrades |

Both powder types share the same upgrade pool — you can use either for most HotM nodes.

## Mining Gear Progression

### Early Mining
- **Armor**: Glacite Armor set or basic miner armor
- **Tool**: Iron/Gold/Diamond Pickaxe → Stonk (mid-game upgrade)
- **Pet**: Lvl 1 Silverfish → Baby Yeti for speed

### Mid Mining (HotM 4–7)
- **Armor**: Divan's Armor (Gemstone Chambers set)
- **Tool**: Titanium Drill DR-X455 → DR-X655 (later upgrade)
- **Pet**: Lvl 100 Legendary Bal (Glacite Tunnels) or Mole
- **Accessory**: Mithril Infusion, Titanium Artifact

### Late / Endgame Mining (HotM 8–10)
- **Armor**: Full Divan's Armor with Perfect Gemstone slots
- **Tool**: Divan's Drill (best in slot)
- **Pet**: Quick Claw (endgame pet for gemstone speed)
- **Reforge**: Lustrous (general) or Refined Titanium (specific builds)

<Warning title="Drills need fuel">
All drill tools require **fuel** (e.g., Volta, Biofuel, Comet Fuel). Keep fuel stocked — an empty drill is a dead tool. The Oil Barrel accessory helps automate this.
</Warning>

## Best Mining Methods

### Gemstone Mining (Crystal Hollows) — Best Coins/hr
Mine Ruby, Sapphire, Amethyst, Jade, Topaz, Amber, and Jasper in the Crystal Hollows. Gems are always in demand for gear upgrades.

- **Coins/hr**: 3–15M (scales heavily with gear)
- **Req**: HotM 4+, Divan's Armor, Gemstone Drill
- **Pet**: Quick Claw or Mole (Legendary)

### Mithril/Powder Farming (Dwarven Mines)
Good early-mid game method for both coins and powder. Mithril is used for HotM upgrades and crafting recipes.

- **Coins/hr**: 1–3M
- **Req**: HotM 2+, basic drill
- **Best if**: You need powder AND coins

### Glacite Tunnel Mining (HotM 8+)
Glacite Ore is used for high-level crafting. The tunnels also have dense ore veins.

- **Coins/hr**: 2–6M
- **Req**: HotM 8, Divan's or better armor

## Mining Fortune (MF) — Key Stat

Mining Fortune multiplies your ore drops. Stack it from:

| Source | MF Bonus |
|--------|---------|
| HotM tree nodes | Up to +250 |
| Divan's Armor | Large per piece |
| Mining pet ability | Varies |
| Equipment (Gauntlet) | +50–100 |
| Reforge: Refined | Moderate |

<Tip title="Fortune > Speed for profit">
Mining Fortune directly multiplies drops. At 300+ MF you get 3× drops from every ore. Speed helps you mine faster, but Fortune is the actual multiplier. Prioritize Fortune nodes in HotM first.
</Tip>
