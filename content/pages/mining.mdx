## Mining Overview

Mining in SkyBlock is a deep progression system built around the **Heart of the Mountain (HotM)** skill tree. Unlike vanilla Minecraft, you earn **Mining XP**, **Powder**, and special resources that scale dramatically with your gear and HotM investment.

<Tip title="Unlock Dwarven Mines first">
To unlock HotM, talk to **Rhys** in the Dwarven Mines and give him 15× any enchanted ore (e.g. Enchanted Cobblestone). Access the Dwarven Mines via the portal in the Hub near the Gold Mine entrance.
</Tip>

---

## Heart of the Mountain (HotM)

HotM is your personal mining skill tree with **10 tiers** and dozens of upgradable nodes. Leveling requires **Mithril Powder** (from Dwarven Mines) and **Gemstone Powder** (from Crystal Hollows).

<TableCard title="HotM Tier Milestones">

| HotM Level | Key Unlock |
|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | Unlock HotM tree |
| 2 | Mining Speed II node |
| 3 | Quick Forge (faster crafting) |
| 4 | Crystal Hollows access |
| 5 | Efficient Miner node |
| 6 | Mining Speed Boost ability |
| 7 | Vein Seeker node |
| 8 | Glacite Tunnels access |
| 9 | Mineshaft access |
| 10 | Full tree, all nodes available |

</TableCard>

<Info title="Token of the Mountain">
Each HotM level gives a **Token of the Mountain** to spend on new nodes. Focus **Mining Speed** and **Mining Fortune** nodes first — they directly multiply your drops and efficiency.
</Info>

<Info title="HotM reset is now free">
As of **July 2025**, resetting your HotM tree costs **0 coins**. You can freely reassign all your node points without any penalty. Respec whenever your playstyle or gear changes.
</Info>

## Powder Types

| Type | Where From | Used For |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Mithril Powder | Dwarven Mines, Glacite Tunnels | HotM upgrades |
| Gemstone Powder | Crystal Hollows | HotM upgrades |

Both types share the same upgrade pool — you can use either for most HotM nodes.

## Mining Gear by Stage

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Early (HotM 1–3)">

- **Armor**: Glacite Armor or basic miner armor
- **Tool**: Iron/Gold/Diamond Pickaxe → Stonk
- **Pet**: Lvl 1 Silverfish for early XP
- **Focus**: Reach HotM 4 to unlock Crystal Hollows

</Tab>
<Tab label="Mid (HotM 4–7)">

- **Armor**: Divan's Armor (Gemstone Chambers set — budget version first)
- **Tool**: Titanium Drill DR-X455 → DR-X655
- **Pet**: Legendary Bal (Glacite Tunnels) or Mole
- **Accessory**: Mithril Infusion, Titanium Artifact
- **Focus**: Build Mining Fortune, grind Crystal Hollows gemstones

</Tab>
<Tab label="Late (HotM 8–10)">

- **Armor**: Full Divan's Armor with Perfect Gemstone slots
- **Tool**: Divan's Drill (best in slot)
- **Pet**: Quick Claw (endgame gemstone speed)
- **Reforge**: Lustrous (general) or Refined Titanium
- **Focus**: Maximize Fortune, push Mineshaft for best XP

</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning title="Drills need fuel">
All drill tools require **fuel** (e.g., Volta, Biofuel, Comet Fuel). An empty drill is a dead tool. The Oil Barrel accessory helps automate fuel management.
</Warning>

## Best Mining Methods

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Gemstone Mining">

**Coins/hr: 3–15M** (scales heavily with gear)

Mine Ruby, Sapphire, Amethyst, Jade, Topaz, Amber, and Jasper in the Crystal Hollows. Gems are always in demand for gear upgrades.

- **Req**: HotM 4+, Divan's Armor, Gemstone Drill
- **Pet**: Quick Claw or Mole (Legendary)
- **Best with**: Cole mayor (+100 Mining Fortune)

</Tab>
<Tab label="Mithril / Powder">

**Coins/hr: 1–3M**

Good early-mid method for both coins and powder. Mithril is used for HotM upgrades and crafting recipes.

- **Req**: HotM 2+, basic drill
- **Best if**: You need Mithril Powder AND coins simultaneously

</Tab>
<Tab label="Glacite Tunnels">

**Coins/hr: 2–6M**

Dense ore veins and Glacite Ore for high-level crafting. Also has Powder spawns.

- **Req**: HotM 8, Divan's or better armor

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Mining Fortune (MF) — Key Stat

Mining Fortune directly multiplies your ore drops. Stack it from every available source.

| Source | MF Bonus |
|--------|---------|
| HotM tree nodes | Up to +250 |
| Divan's Armor | Large per piece |
| Mining pet ability | Varies |
| Equipment (Gauntlet) | +50–100 |
| Reforge: Refined | Moderate |
| Cole mayor perk | +100 (temporary) |

<Tip title="Fortune > Speed for profit">
At 300+ MF you get 3× drops from every ore. Speed helps you mine faster, but Fortune is the actual profit multiplier. Prioritize Fortune nodes in HotM first, then supplement with speed.
</Tip>
