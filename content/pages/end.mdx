## You're in End Game If…

- You clear **Dungeons Floor 7** or **Master Mode M1+**
- You have **500+ Magical Power**
- You earn **10M+ coins/hr** in your main lane
- You have a **Legendary pet at Level 100** in your primary method

End game is about **optimization** — squeezing more out of what you have, upgrading specific gear pieces, and pushing into Master Mode dungeons and Kuudra.

<Info title="Reforges matter more at endgame">
At endgame your **reforge** can add 5–15% damage. For Berserk: **Withered** on armor and weapon. For Archer: **Necrotic** armor + **Spiritual** weapon. For Mage: **Ancient** armor.
</Info>

## Master Mode Dungeons

Master Mode (M1–M7) is the endgame dungeon content. Requires Catacombs 30+ for M1.

<TableCard title="Master Mode Floors">

| Floor | Cata Req. | Notable Drops |
|-------|----------|---------------|
| M1 | 30 | Recombobulator 3000, coins |
| M2 | 32 | Recombobulator, T12 enchant books |
| M3 | 34 | Master Skull T4, coins |
| M4 | 36 | Master Skull T5, Obsidian Chest |
| M5 | 38 | Starred gear — coins/hr peak |
| M6 | 40 | High-value starred items |
| M7 | 42 | Wither Essence farming, top-end items |

</TableCard>

<Warning title="Party composition is critical in Master Mode">
Master Mode requires coordinated parties. A Healer becomes near-mandatory at M4+. Solo players struggle significantly without one.
</Warning>

## Kuudra — The Other Endgame

[Kuudra](/kuudra) is the co-op boss in the Crimson Isle. The main alternative to Dungeons for endgame coins/hr. Rewards **Crimson Essence**, **Kuudra tokens**, and rare attribute gear.

<TableCard title="Kuudra Tier Overview">

| Tier | Difficulty | Reward Focus |
|------|-----------|--------------|
| T1 Basic | Easy | Learning mechanics |
| T2 Hot | Moderate | Good introduction |
| T3 Burning | Hard | Profit starts here |
| T4 Fiery | Very Hard | Best coins/hr with setup |
| T5 Infernal | Extreme | Best overall rewards |

</TableCard>

<Tip title="Getting from 600 → 700 MP">
This jump requires upgrading many accessories to higher rarities. Focus on **Epic → Legendary** upgrades rather than adding new accessories. Recombobulator 3000 is the fastest path here.
</Tip>
