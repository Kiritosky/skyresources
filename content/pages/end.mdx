## You're in End Game If…

- You clear **Dungeons Floor 7 / Master Mode M1+**
- You have **500+ Magic Power**
- You earn **10M+ coins/hr** in your main lane
- You have a **Legendary pet at Level 100** in your main method

End game in SkyBlock is about **optimization** — squeezing more out of what you have, upgrading specific gear pieces, and pushing into Master Mode dungeons and Kuudra.

<Info title="Reforges matter more here">
At endgame, your **reforge** can add 5–15% damage. For Berserk: **Withered** on armor and weapon. For Archer: **Necrotic** armor + **Spiritual** or **Light** weapon. For Mage: **Ancient** armor.
</Info>

## Master Mode Dungeons

Master Mode (M1–M7) is the endgame dungeon content. Catacombs Level 30+ is needed for M1; M7 requires Catacombs 40+.

| Floor | Cata Req. | Notable Drops |
|-------|----------|---------------|
| M1 | 30 | Recombobulator 3000, good coins |
| M2 | 33 | Recombobulator, T12 enchant books |
| M3 | 35 | Master Skull T4, coins |
| M4 | 37 | Master Skull T5, Obsidian chest |
| M5 | 38 | Starred gear, coins/hr peaks |
| M6 | 40 | High-value starred items |
| M7 | 43 | Wither Essence farming, high-end items |

<Warning title="Party composition is critical in M mode">
Master Mode requires coordinated parties. A Healer becomes almost mandatory at M4+. Solo players can struggle significantly without one.
</Warning>

## Kuudra — The Other Endgame

[Kuudra](/kuudra) is the co-op boss in the Crimson Isle. It's the main alternative to Dungeons for endgame coins/hr. Kuudra rewards **Crimson Essence**, **Kuudra tokens**, and rare attribute gear.

| Tier | Difficulty | Reward Focus |
|------|-----------|--------------|
| T1 Basic | Easy | Learning the mechanic |
| T2 Hot | Moderate | Good introduction |
| T3 Burning | Hard | Profit starts here |
| T4 Fiery | Very Hard | Best coins/hr with setup |
| T5 Infernal | Extreme | Best overall rewards |

## Magic Power — Endgame Targets

| MP Range | Status |
|----------|--------|
| 500 | Mid-game standard, functional |
| 600 | Good endgame baseline |
| 700 | Strong — most players target this |
| 800 | Excellent — significant investment |
| 900+ | Min-maxed — diminishing returns |

<Tip title="Getting from 600 → 700 MP">
The jump from 600 to 700 MP requires upgrading many accessories to higher rarities. Focus on upgrading **Epic → Legendary** talismans rather than adding new ones. Recombobulator 3000 is your best friend here.
</Tip>

## What to Focus On

1. **Push Catacombs** to 35–40 to unlock M3–M5.
2. **Get Necron's armor** — the standard endgame dungeon set.
3. **Build Kuudra** — unlock T3 for profit, T4 for endgame coins.
4. **Upgrade accessories** — hit 700+ MP.
5. **Max farming** — Farming 60 gives +242 Health free.
6. **Get a Legendary pet** for your main method at max level.
