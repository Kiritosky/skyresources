## What Are Accessories?

Accessories (also called **Talismans, Rings, Orbs, Artifacts, Relics**) are special items equipped in your **Accessory Bag**. They give passive stat bonuses and contribute **Magical Power (MP)** to your profile.

Accessories do **not** take up inventory space — they go in your Accessory Bag (open via the Backpack icon in your inventory).

---

## Magical Power (MP)

**Magical Power** is the single most important stat from accessories. It is a cumulative score calculated from all your equipped accessories, and it scales your combat stats via your chosen **Power Stone**.

### MP per Rarity

| Rarity | MP per accessory |
|--------|----------------|
| Common | 3 |
| Uncommon | 5 |
| Rare | 8 |
| Epic | 12 |
| Legendary | 16 |
| Mythic | 22 |
| Special | 3 |
| Very Special | 5 |

</TableCard>

<Warning title="No duplicate MP">
Each **unique accessory family** only counts its highest-rarity version toward MP. If you have a Haste Ring (Rare) and a Haste Artifact (Epic), only the Epic version contributes MP. Remove duplicates — they waste bag slots.
</Warning>

## How MP Scales Your Stats

MP scales stats using a **logarithmic formula** — not fixed breakpoints. Every point of MP you gain contributes to your stat bonus, but the gain per point decreases as your total MP grows higher.

This means:
- Going from **0 → 300 MP** gives the biggest relative impact per coin spent
- Going from **700 → 800 MP** still helps, but costs far more to achieve the same relative gain
- There are **no hard "breakpoints"** — stats increase continuously as MP increases

<Tip title="Aim for 300 MP first">
The early MP range (0–300) gives the best return on investment. Filling your bag with mid-rarity accessories before chasing rare upgrades is almost always the right call.
</Tip>

## Accessory Bag Upgrades

Your Accessory Bag starts small and expands via:
- **Community Shop** (Hub NPC) — buy bag upgrades with coins
- **Profile upgrades** — some slots from SkyBlock Level milestones
- **Collections** — certain collection milestones grant bag space

| Stage | Bag Slots |
|-------|----------|
| Early game | 15–25 |
| Mid game | 45–60 |
| Late game | 75–100 |
| Endgame | 100+ |

## Power Stones

A **Power Stone** is a special accessory that determines how your MP converts to stats. Equip one at a time via the star icon in your Accessory Bag interface.

<TableCard title="Power Stones by Playstyle">

| Power Stone | Bonus Focus | Best For |
|-------------|------------|---------|
| Sorrow | Strength | Berserk / Combat |
| Fierce | Crit Damage | Archer / DPS |
| Spiritual | Speed + Intelligence | Mage / movement |
| Silky | Farming Fortune | Farming builds |
| Precise | Crit Chance | Early-mid players |
| Bubba | Fishing Fortune | Fishing builds |
| Bizarre | Magic Find | Farming for drops |

</TableCard>

<Info title="Check your Power Stone">
Many players forget to change their Power Stone when switching content. Use SkyHanni's alert system or make it part of your routine when changing activities.
</Info>

## Tuning

**Tuning** lets you spend **Tuning Points** (earned from Magical Power milestones and certain quests) to permanently boost specific stats. Points are limited — choose carefully.

| Activity | Recommended Tuning |
|----------|-------------------|
| Dungeons Mage | Intelligence → Ability Damage |
| Dungeons Archer | Crit Damage → Strength |
| Farming | Farming Fortune |
| Mining | Mining Fortune → Speed |

You can reset tuning but it costs coins. Avoid wasting points on stats you won't use.

---

## Recombobulator 3000

The **Recombobulator 3000** upgrades an accessory's rarity by one tier (e.g. Rare → Epic). It's one of the most powerful tools for boosting MP.

- Bought from the **Dark Auction** or the Auction House
- Costs roughly **15–30M coins** per Recombobulator
- Best used on accessories you plan to keep long-term
- **Always upgrade your highest-rarity pieces first** — a Legendary → Mythic upgrade (+6 MP) gives 3× more MP per Recombobulator than a Common → Uncommon (+2 MP)

<Warning title="Don't waste Recombobulators on low-rarity pieces">
Upgrading Common → Uncommon (+2 MP) costs the same Recombobulator as Legendary → Mythic (+6 MP). Always apply to the highest-rarity piece you have.
</Warning>

## Checklist: Building Your Accessory Bag

<Checklist title="MP optimization steps">
- Remove all duplicate accessories (keep highest rarity only)
- Fill empty slots with any accessible accessories you can find
- Equip a Power Stone that matches your current activity
- Set your Tuning Points — don't leave them unspent
- Recombobulate your highest-rarity pieces when you can afford it
- Aim for 300 MP before buying expensive individual upgrades
- Use SkyCrypt to audit your MP and find easy-to-fill gaps
</Checklist>
