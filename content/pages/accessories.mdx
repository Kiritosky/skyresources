## What Are Accessories?

Accessories (also called **Talismans, Rings, Orbs, Artifacts, Relics**) are special items you equip in your **Accessory Bag**. They give passive stat bonuses and contribute **Magic Power (MP)** to your profile.

Accessories do **not** take up inventory space — they go in your Accessory Bag (opened via the Backpack icon in your inventory).

---

## Magic Power (MP)

**Magic Power** is the single most important stat from accessories. It's a cumulative power score that boosts all your combat stats via your chosen **Power Stone**.

### MP per Rarity

| Rarity | MP per accessory |
|--------|----------------|
| Common | 3 |
| Uncommon | 5 |
| Rare | 8 |
| Epic | 12 |
| Legendary | 16 |
| Mythic | 22 |
| Special | 3 |
| Very Special | 5 |

<Warning title="No duplicate MP">
Each **unique accessory family** only counts its highest-rarity version toward MP. If you have a Haste Ring (Rare) and a Haste Artifact (Epic), only the Epic version contributes MP. Remove duplicates — they waste bag space.
</Warning>

## MP Breakpoints

MP gives bonus stats at every 10-point milestone. The most important breakpoints are:

| MP | Effect |
|----|--------|
| 10 | First bonus |
| 100 | Moderate stat boost |
| 200 | Good baseline |
| 300 | **Major bonus — priority target** |
| 400 | Strong |
| 500 | **Big damage multiplier — second priority** |
| 600 | Very strong |
| 700 | Common endgame target |
| 800 | Excellent — significant cost |
| 900+ | Min-maxed |

<Tip title="Hit 300 MP first">
If you're below 300 MP, focus there first. The 300 MP milestone gives one of the largest single jumps in combat power in the game. Fill your bag with any accessories you can afford to reach it.
</Tip>

## Accessory Bag Upgrades

Your Accessory Bag starts small and expands over time. Bag upgrades come from:
- **Community Shop** (Hub NPC) — buy with coins
- **Profile upgrades** — some bag slots are unlocked through profile progression
- **Collections** — certain collection milestones grant bag space

### Approximate bag sizes by stage:
| Stage | Bag Slots |
|-------|----------|
| Early game | 15–25 |
| Mid game | 45–60 |
| Late game | 75–100 |
| Endgame | 100+ |

## Power Stones

A **Power Stone** is a special accessory that determines how your MP converts to stats. You equip one at a time via your Accessory Bag interface.

| Power Stone | Bonus Focus | Best For |
|-------------|------------|---------|
| Sorrow | Strength | Berserk / Combat |
| Fierce | Crit Damage | Archer / DPS |
| Spiritual | Speed + Int | Mage / movement |
| Silky | Farming Fortune | Farming |
| Precise | Crit Chance | Early-mid players |
| Bubba | Fishing Fortune | Fishing builds |

<Info title="Check your Power Stone">
Go to your Accessory Bag → click the star icon → choose Power Stone. Make sure it matches your playstyle. Many players forget to change it when switching content.
</Info>

## Tuning

**Tuning** lets you spend **Tuning Points** (earned from HP milestones and certain quests) to permanently boost stats. You choose which stat to put each point into.

| Activity | Recommended Tuning |
|----------|-------------------|
| Dungeons Mage | Intelligence → Ability Damage |
| Dungeons Archer | Crit Damage → Strength |
| Farming | Farming Fortune |
| Mining | Mining Fortune → Speed |

Tuning points are limited — choose carefully. You can reset tuning but it costs coins.

---

## Recombobulator 3000

The **Recombobulator 3000** upgrades an accessory's rarity by one tier (e.g. Rare → Epic). It's one of the most powerful tools for boosting MP.

- Bought from the **Dark Auction** or player Auction House
- Currently costs ~15–30M coins per Recombobulator
- Best used on accessories you plan to keep long-term
- Priority: Upgrade your highest-MP accessories first (Legendary → Mythic if possible)

<Warning title="Don't waste Recombobulators on common accessories">
Upgrading a Common accessory (3 MP → 5 MP = +2 MP) costs the same Recombobulator as upgrading a Legendary to Mythic (16 MP → 22 MP = +6 MP). Always upgrade the highest rarity piece you have.
</Warning>

## Reforging Accessories

Reforging your accessories can give meaningful bonuses. The **Warped** reforge gives the most Magic Power from Enchanting/Combat stats per piece.

| Reforge | Best For |
|---------|---------|
| Warped | Magic Find + damage (most popular) |
| Itchy | Crit Damage builds |
| Lucky | Crit Chance builds |
| Spiked | Strength builds |

Most players use **Warped** as their default accessory reforge unless specifically building for one stat.
