## What is the Garden?

The **Garden** is your personal farming island, separate from your Private Island. It gives a **3× Farming XP multiplier**, access to Farming Fortune upgrades, crop milestone progression, pest farming, and Jacob's Farming Contest.

<Info title="How to unlock the Garden">
The Garden is unlocked through the **Farming Island quest chain**. Talk to the Farming NPC in the Hub to start. You'll need basic farming gear and some starter crops.
</Info>

---

## Farming Fortune (FF)

**Farming Fortune** is the most important farming stat. It directly multiplies your crop drops.

At **100 FF** you get approximately 2× drops. At **300 FF** approximately 4× drops. Stacking FF is the fastest way to increase your coins/hr.

### FF Sources

<TableCard title="Farming Fortune Sources">

| Source | FF Amount |
|--------|----------|
| Farming Skill (each level) | +1 per level |
| Garden Level (each level) | +5 per level |
| Hoe reforge (Blessed) | Up to +75 |
| Farming armor set (Melon, Cropie, etc.) | +100–200+ |
| Pet (Elephant / Rabbit) | +50–120 |
| Equipment (Gauntlet / Helmet) | +50–100 |
| Silex (hoe upgrade) | +50 per application |

</TableCard>

<Tip title="Armor gives the most FF">
Your **armor set** contributes the most Farming Fortune by far. Farming Suit sets (Melon, Cropie, Mushroom, Squash, Fermento) are built specifically for FF. Upgrade your armor before worrying about pet level.
</Tip>

## Crop Milestones

Each crop has its own milestone progression. Hitting milestones unlocks:
- **Equipment slots** on your farming armor
- Permanent **Farming Fortune** bonuses
- Cosmetic rewards
- **Crop Upgrade** items (Lotus, Jacob's Ticket exchanges)

Push milestones in the crops you farm most. Milestones 1–5 are fast; 6–9 require sustained grinding.

## Pest Farming

Pests are small enemies that spawn in your Garden while farming. Killing them drops rare items and gives bonuses.

**Pest farming setup (endgame):**

<Checklist title="Pest farming requirements">
- High Farming Fortune (1000+) to farm fast and spawn pests often
- Vacuum (Turbo Potato or Squash Vacuum) to collect pests
- Pest Bestiary leveled for bonus XP
- Greenhouse upgrade unlocked in your Garden
- Mushroom or Nether Wart as primary crop (best pest density)
</Checklist>

Endgame pest farming earns **20–40M coins/hr** with a fully optimized setup.

<Info title="Pests require Greenhouse unlock">
The Greenhouse is a Garden upgrade that enables the pest mechanic. Complete the Greenhouse quest and purchase the upgrade before pests will spawn.
</Info>

## Jacob's Farming Contest

Jacob's Contest runs every **20 SkyBlock minutes** (~20 real minutes). Farm as much of the designated crop as possible in the window.

Medals earned (Bronze, Silver, Gold) buy:
- **Golden Tickets** (opens farming shop)
- **Farming equipment** upgrades
- Rare cosmetics

<Tip title="Contest prep">
Before the contest: clear your inventory, equip your best hoe + farming pet + full FF gear. The goal is raw speed — not managing inventory or selling mid-contest.
</Tip>

## Visitors

Visitors are NPCs in your Garden who offer trade contracts. They ask for specific crops in exchange for:
- Coins
- Farming XP
- **Copper** (Garden shop currency)
- Rare equipment upgrades

Accept visitors offering good XP/coin value per crop. Skip visitors asking for crops you don't farm. Visitors rotate over time.
