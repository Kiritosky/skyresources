## What is the Garden?

The **Garden** is your personal farming island, separate from your Private Island. It gives access to:
- **Farming Fortune (FF)** upgrades exclusive to the Garden
- **Crop Milestones** — progression that unlocks gear slots, stats, and boosts
- **Pest Farming** — endgame farming mechanic with high coins/hr
- **Jacob's Farming Contest** — competitive farming events with exclusive rewards
- **Visitors** — NPCs that offer trade contracts for crops

<Info title="How to unlock the Garden">
The Garden is unlocked through the **Farming Island quest chain**. Talk to the Farming NPC in the Hub to start. You'll need basic farming gear and some starter crops.
</Info>

## Farming Fortune (FF)

**Farming Fortune** is the most important farming stat. It directly multiplies your crop drops.

At **100 FF**, you get ~2× drops. At **300 FF**, ~4× drops. Stacking FF is the fastest way to increase your coins/hr.

### FF Sources

| Source | FF Amount |
|--------|----------|
| Farming Skill (each level) | +1 per level |
| Garden Level (each level) | +5 per level |
| Hoe reforge (Blessed) | Up to +75 |
| Armor set (Melon/Cropie etc.) | +100–200+ |
| Pet (Elephant/Rabbit) | +50–120 |
| Equipment (Gauntlet/Helmet) | +50–100 |
| Silex (hoe upgrade) | +50 per application |
| Lotus/Daedalus upgrades | Varies |

<Tip title="Gear > Pet for FF">
Your **armor set** gives the most FF. Farming Suit sets (Melon, Cropie, Mushroom, Squash, Fermento) are stacked specifically for FF. Upgrade your armor before worrying about pet levels.
</Tip>

## Crop Milestones

Each crop has its own milestone progression. Hitting milestones unlocks:
- **Equipment slots** on your farming armor
- Permanent **Farming Fortune** bonuses
- Cosmetic rewards
- **Crop Upgrade** items (Lotus, Jacob's Ticket exchanges)

Push milestones in the crops you farm most. Milestone 1–5 is fast; 6–9 requires sustained farming.

## Pest Farming

Pests are small enemies that spawn in your Garden while farming. Killing them drops:
- **Pest drops** (rare items, specific to pest type)
- **Garden XP** bonuses
- **Farming XP** multipliers via the Pest Bestiary

**Pest farming setup** (endgame):
- High FF (1000+) to farm fast and spawn pests often
- **Vacuum** (Turbo Potato or Squash Vacuum) to collect pests
- **Pest Bestiary** leveled for bonus XP
- Mushroom and Nether Wart are top pest farming crops

Endgame pest farming earns **20–40M coins/hr** with a fully optimized setup.

<Info title="Pests require Greenhouse unlock">
The Greenhouse is a Garden upgrade that unlocks the pest mechanic. Complete the Greenhouse quest first, then purchase the Greenhouse upgrade to enable pests.
</Info>

## Jacob's Farming Contest

Jacob's Contest is a farming competition that runs every **20 SkyBlock minutes** (real-time). You farm as much of the designated crop as possible in the window.

Medals earned (Bronze, Silver, Gold) are used to buy:
- **Golden Ticket** (opens farming shop)
- **Farming equipment** upgrades
- Rare cosmetics

<Tip title="Contest prep">
Before the contest starts, make sure your inventory is clear for drops. Use your best hoe + farming pet + full FF gear. The goal is raw speed — not selling or managing inventory.
</Tip>

## Visitors

Visitors are NPCs that offer trade contracts in your Garden. They ask for specific crops in exchange for rewards like:
- **Coins**
- **Farming XP**
- **Copper** (used in Garden Shop)
- **Rare equipment upgrades**

Accept visitors that give good XP/coin value per crop. Skip visitors asking for crops you don't farm. Visitors rotate over time.
