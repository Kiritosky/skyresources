## What Are Dungeons?

Dungeons are SkyBlock's co-op endgame PvE content. You enter the **Catacombs** dungeon with up to 5 players, complete puzzles, fight minibosses, and kill a floor boss. Each floor has unique mechanics, gear drops, and a leaderboard for speed.

There are **7 Normal floors** (F1–F7) and **7 Master Mode floors** (M1–M7). Master Mode requires clearing F7 first and is significantly harder.

<Info title="Dungeon Skill">
Each dungeon run gives **Catacombs XP** toward your Dungeon Skill level. Higher Catacombs level is required for later floors (and Master Mode) and gives stat bonuses to your dungeon performance.
</Info>

<Warning title="Catacombs requirement is a gate">
You literally cannot enter a floor if you are below the Catacombs level requirement. Grind lower floors until you reach the threshold — there are no shortcuts.
</Warning>

## Dungeon Classes

Each player picks a class before entering. Your class level scales your class bonus.

| Class | Role | Passive Bonus | Best For |
|-------|------|--------------|---------|
| Archer | DPS | Bow damage | Consistent ranged DPS |
| Berserk | Melee DPS | Sword damage | AoE clear, strong single target |
| Mage | Ability DPS | Ability damage + Int | Hyperion, Juju, Mage builds |
| Healer | Support | Heal teammates | Keeping the party alive at high floors |
| Tank | Frontline | Damage reduction | Absorbing boss hits |

<Tip title="Level all classes eventually">
You can switch classes freely between runs. Leveling every class increases your **class average** which boosts overall dungeon stats. Running a second class occasionally while farming lower floors is efficient.
</Tip>

## Dungeon Gear by Stage

### Early Dungeons (F1–F3)
Use whatever you have — Hardened Diamond, Golem armor, basic swords. Focus on completing floors, not optimizing.

### Mid Dungeons (F4–F5)
- **Armor**: Strong Dragon → Shadow Assassin (F4 drop)
- **Weapon**: Giant's Sword / Aspect of the End → Livid Dagger (F5 drop)
- **Pet**: Lvl 100 Rare Tiger or Spider

### Late Dungeons (F6–F7)
- **Armor**: Necron's Armor (from F6/F7) — best in class
- **Weapon**: Hyperion (Mage), Livid Dagger / Juju (Archer), Shadow Fury (Berserk)
- **Pet**: Legendary Ender Dragon (Mage), Legendary Tiger (Berserk), Legendary Baby Yeti (Archer)

### Master Mode (M1–M7)
- Same gear as F7 but everything needs to be starred (☆) and enchanted to max
- Healer becomes near-mandatory at M4+
- **Recommended**: Golden Head for revives, full Necron starred, 600+ MP

## Secrets — A Major Score Multiplier

Each floor has hidden **secrets** (chests, bats, items). Finding more secrets increases your score, which affects:
- The quality of your chest roll at the end
- Your **S/S+** clear status (needed for best rewards)

<Info title="Secrets tracker">
The **SkyHanni** mod has a built-in secrets tracker that shows secret locations on your screen during runs. Use it to hit S+ score consistently.
</Info>

## Catacombs XP Guide

To level up Catacombs fast:

1. **Run the highest floor you can complete reliably.**
2. **S/S+ scores** give more XP than D/C scores.
3. **Master Mode M1** gives significantly more XP per run than F7.
4. **Don't skip floors** — each floor you clear unlocks the next permanently.
