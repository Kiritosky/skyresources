## What Are Dungeons?

Dungeons are SkyBlock's co-op endgame PvE content. Up to 5 players enter the **Catacombs**, complete puzzles, fight minibosses, and kill a floor boss. Each floor has unique mechanics, gear drops, and a leaderboard for speed.

There are **7 Normal floors** (F1–F7) and **7 Master Mode floors** (M1–M7). Master Mode requires clearing F7 first and is significantly harder.

<Info title="Catacombs Skill">
Every run gives **Catacombs XP** toward your Dungeon Skill level. Higher Catacombs level unlocks later floors and gives stat bonuses inside dungeons.
</Info>

<Warning title="Catacombs requirement is a hard gate">
You cannot enter a floor if you are below the Catacombs level requirement. Grind lower floors until you hit the threshold — there are no workarounds.
</Warning>

## Dungeon Classes

Each player picks a class before entering. Your class level scales your class-specific bonus.

<TableCard title="Dungeon Classes">

| Class | Role | Passive Bonus | Best For |
|-------|------|--------------|---------|
| Archer | DPS | Bow damage | Consistent ranged DPS |
| Berserk | Melee DPS | Sword damage | AoE clear, strong single target |
| Mage | Ability DPS | Ability damage + Intelligence | Hyperion, Juju, spell builds |
| Healer | Support | Heal teammates | Keeping the party alive at high floors |
| Tank | Frontline | Damage reduction | Absorbing boss hits |

</TableCard>

<Tip title="Level all classes eventually">
You can switch classes between runs for free. Leveling every class raises your **class average** which boosts overall dungeon stats. Running a secondary class on lower floors is efficient.
</Tip>

## Gear by Stage

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Early (F1–F3)">

Use whatever you have — Hardened Diamond, Golem armor, basic swords. Focus on completing floors and learning mechanics, not optimizing.

**Goal**: Reach Catacombs 12 to unlock F4.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Mid (F4–F5)">

- **Armor**: Strong Dragon → Shadow Assassin (F4 drop)
- **Weapon**: Giant's Sword / AotE → Livid Dagger (F5 drop)
- **Pet**: Lvl 100 Rare Tiger or Spider

**Goal**: Clear F5 reliably and push to Catacombs 24.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Late (F6–F7)">

- **Armor**: Necron's Armor — best in class for F6/F7
- **Weapon**: Hyperion (Mage), Livid Dagger / Juju (Archer), Shadow Fury (Berserk)
- **Pet**: Legendary Ender Dragon (Mage), Tiger (Berserk), Baby Yeti (Archer)

**Goal**: Farm F7 for Wither items and push to Catacombs 30 for M1.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Master Mode">

- Same gear as F7 but every piece should be **starred (☆)** and max enchanted
- Healer becomes near-mandatory at M4+
- **Recommended extras**: Golden Head for revives, 600+ Magical Power

**Goal**: M5 for best coins/hr; M7 for Wither Essence endgame farming.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Secrets — A Major Score Multiplier

Each floor has hidden **secrets** (chests, bats, items on the ground). Finding more secrets increases your run score, which affects:
- The quality of your chest roll at the end
- Whether you achieve **S/S+** (needed for best rewards)

<Info title="Use SkyHanni for secrets">
The **SkyHanni** mod shows secret locations on your screen during runs automatically. It's the single biggest quality-of-life improvement for dungeon players.
</Info>

## Run Score Breakdown

| Category | Max Points | How to Maximize |
|----------|-----------|----------------|
| Exploration | 60 | Find all rooms + secrets |
| Speed | 40 | Finish fast (under 20 min for S) |
| Skill | 40 | Low deaths + high mob kill % |
| Bonus | Varies | Kill crypts, treasure hunter perk |

<Warning title="Deaths tank your score">
A single death on F7 can drop you from S+ to S. Prioritize survivability, especially when learning new floors. Don't bring gear you can't survive with.
</Warning>
