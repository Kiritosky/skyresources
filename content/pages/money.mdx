## How to Pick a Method

The best method is the one you will **consistently do**. Every method on this page can be viable — what matters is:
1. Matching your **game stage** (gear requirements)
2. Matching your **playstyle** (AFK, active, social)
3. **Sticking to it** long enough to optimize

<Tip title="Consistency beats chasing the meta">
Many players earn 30–50% less than they could because they switch methods every patch. Pick one lane, learn its nuances, and compound your gains. A well-optimized method beats a marginally better one you haven't mastered.
</Tip>

## Farming — The Accessible Lane

Garden farming is the most accessible high-income method. You don't need a team, it's relaxing, and it scales smoothly.

**Getting started:**
1. Unlock the Garden (early-game quest)
2. Stack **Farming Fortune** (FF) — this is the main multiplier
3. Use **Elephant or Rabbit** legendary pet at Level 100
4. Pick one crop and push milestones in it

**Best crops for coins:**
- **Mushroom** — best coins/hr endgame, complex setup
- **Nether Wart** (Crimson Isle) — very high value, needs Crimson access
- **Pumpkin** — reliable Bazaar price, easy to set up
- **Melon** — excellent with high FF

## Mining — The Scalable Lane

Mining scales harder than farming but needs more upfront investment.

**Getting started:**
1. Unlock Dwarven Mines, level HotM to 4+
2. Get a basic drill and Divan's Armor (budget version first)
3. Grind Crystal Hollows gemstones

**Scales with**: HotM level, drill quality, Mining Fortune, Divan's gem slots.

---

## Dungeons — The Social Lane

Dungeons reward teamwork and skill. M5+ is one of the best coins/hr for social players.

**Getting started:**
1. Push Catacombs to 30 via F7
2. Build Necron's Armor
3. Find a consistent party (use Discord communities)

## Flipping & Bazaar Trading

Flipping means buying low and selling high. No gear needed — just time, knowledge, and capital.

| Method                                | Risk     | Skill needed |
|---------------------------------------|----------|--------------|
| Bazaar instant buy/sell spread        | Low      | Very low     |
| Bazaar flip (buy order → sell)        | Low-Med  | Low          |
| Auction House flipping                | Med-High | Medium       |
| Mayor prep (stock items before mayor) | Medium   | Medium-High  |

<Info title="Flipping resources">
[COFL](https://sky.coflnet.com/) has a built-in flipper with live margin data. Start with Bazaar flips (buy order at -0.1%, sell order at +0.1%) — very low risk with consistent small margins.
</Info>
