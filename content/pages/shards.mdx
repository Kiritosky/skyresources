## What is the Bestiary?

The **Bestiary** tracks how many of each mob type you've killed on your profile. Killing mobs fills the Bestiary progress for that creature. Reaching milestones unlocks:
- **Magic Find** bonuses (increases rare drop chance)
- Permanent **stat bonuses**
- **SkyBlock XP**
- Cosmetic titles

Opening your Bestiary: **SkyBlock Menu → Bestiary** (or use SkyHanni's keybind).

---

## How Shards Work

**Bestiary Shards** are items dropped by mobs that contribute double progress to their Bestiary entry. They're primarily obtained from:
- Rare mob drops
- Special event mobs
- Boss mob variants

You don't *need* shards to complete the Bestiary — killing mobs normally fills progress — but shards speed it up.

---

## Magic Find from Bestiary

**Magic Find** increases the chance of rare drops from all sources. Every 10 Bestiary levels gives +1 Magic Find.

| Bestiary Milestone | Magic Find Gained |
|-------------------|------------------|
| Every 10 mob kills milestone | +1 MF |
| Level 100 family completion | +5 MF bonus |
| Full Bestiary completed | Large MF reward |

<Tip title="Focus on high-density mobs first">
Mobs you'll kill naturally (Zombies, Skeletons, Spiders in early game) fill Bestiary fast. Don't grind niche mobs specifically for Bestiary early on — focus on the mobs you're already farming.
</Tip>

## Bestiary Families

Each mob belongs to a **family** (e.g. Undead, Arthropod, Ender, Boss). Completing a family gives a family-completion bonus.

| Family | Key Mobs | Bonus |
|--------|---------|-------|
| Undead | Zombies, Wither Skeletons | Magic Find |
| Arthropod | Spiders, Cave Spiders | Magic Find |
| Ender | Endermen, Endermites | Magic Find |
| Blaze | Blazes, Pigmen | Defense |
| Boss | Slayer bosses | Large MF |
| Sea | Sea creatures, fish | Magic Find |

## Efficient Mob Grinding Routes

### Early Game

| Location | Mobs | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| Spider's Den | Spiders, Cave Spiders | Fast to kill, good Arthropod XP |
| Hub Graveyard | Zombies | Easy kills, Undead family |
| Blazing Fortress | Blazes | Good for Blaze family |

### Mid Game

| Location | Mobs | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| The End | Endermen | Ender family + Combat XP |
| Crimson Isle | Blazes, Pigmen | Crimson-exclusive mobs |
| Dungeons | All types | Most families at once |

### Late Game

| Location | Mobs | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| Dungeons M5–M7 | All boss mobs | Boss family + Combat XP |
| Crystal Hollows | Unique mobs | Hollows-specific Bestiary |
| Kuudra runs | Kuudra-specific | Unique entries |

## Bestiary Level Milestones

Each mob entry has 5 tiers. Completing all 5 tiers of a mob gives the maximum bonus for it.

| Tier | Kills Required (varies by mob) | Reward |
|------|-------------------------------|--------|
| 1 | ~100 | Small stat bonus |
| 2 | ~500 | Small MF |
| 3 | ~2,000 | Moderate MF |
| 4 | ~10,000 | Good MF bonus |
| 5 | ~50,000 | Full completion bonus |

<Warning title="Rare mob numbers are high">
Some rare mobs require thousands of kills to complete Bestiary tier 5. Don't try to rush these — focus on common mobs while doing other activities.
</Warning>

## Session Tips

- **Track Bestiary progress** with SkyHanni — it shows how many kills to next tier.
- **Play your normal content** — dungeons, slayers, and farming all passively fill Bestiary.
- **Set aside one session** per week for a dedicated mob grind in a low-traffic area.
- **Use the right pet** for magic find when farming Bestiary (Legendary Black Cat or similar).
