## What is the Bestiary?

The **Bestiary** tracks how many of each mob type you've killed on your profile. Reaching kill milestones unlocks:
- **Magic Find** bonuses — increases rare drop chance from all sources
- Permanent **stat bonuses**
- **SkyBlock XP**
- Cosmetic titles

Open your Bestiary: **SkyBlock Menu → Bestiary** (or use SkyHanni's keybind).

---

## How Shards Work

**Bestiary Shards** are special items dropped by certain mobs that contribute **double progress** to their Bestiary entry. They're obtained from:
- Rare mob drops
- Special event mobs
- Boss mob variants

You don't *need* shards — killing mobs normally fills progress — but shards speed it up significantly.

## Magic Find from Bestiary

Magic Find increases rare drop chance from all sources. Every 10 Bestiary kill milestones gives +1 Magic Find.

<Tip title="Focus on high-density mobs first">
Mobs you kill naturally (Zombies, Skeletons, Spiders in early game) fill Bestiary fast. Don't grind niche mobs specifically — focus on what you're already farming.

## Bestiary Families

Each mob belongs to a **family**. Completing a family (all 5 tiers of every mob in it) gives a family completion bonus.

<TableCard title="Bestiary Families">

| Family | Key Mobs | Completion Bonus |
|--------|---------|-----------------|
| Undead | Zombies, Wither Skeletons | Magic Find |
| Arthropod | Spiders, Cave Spiders | Magic Find |
| Ender | Endermen, Endermites | Magic Find |
| Blaze | Blazes, Pigmen | Defense |
| Boss | Slayer bosses | Large Magic Find |
| Sea | Sea creatures, fish | Magic Find |
| Rift | Vampire, Rift mobs | Motes bonus |

</TableCard>

## Bestiary Tier Requirements

Each mob entry has **5 tiers**. Completing all 5 gives maximum bonus for that mob.

| Tier | Approx. Kills Required | Reward |
|------|----------------------|--------|
| 1 | ~100 | Small stat bonus |
| 2 | ~500 | Small Magic Find |
| 3 | ~2,000 | Moderate Magic Find |
| 4 | ~10,000 | Good Magic Find bonus |
| 5 | ~50,000 | Full completion bonus |

<Warning title="Rare mob kills are a long grind">
Some rare mobs require tens of thousands of kills to complete Tier 5. Don't rush these — fill them naturally while doing other content. Use Bestiary progress as a secondary goal, not your primary activity.
</Warning>

## Efficient Bestiary Tips

<Checklist title="Bestiary efficiency habits">
- Install SkyHanni — it shows kills to next Bestiary tier in real time
- Play your normal content — dungeons, slayers, and farming all passively fill Bestiary
- Use a Legendary Black Cat pet when specifically farming for Magic Find drops
- Focus on the Boss family early — slayer bosses fill it fast and give large MF bonuses
- Set aside weekly sessions for dedicated mob grinding at low-traffic times
</Checklist>

## FAQ

<Details title="Does Bestiary progress carry between profiles?">
No — Bestiary is per-profile. Kill counts do not transfer. If you start a new profile, you start from zero.
</Details>

<Details title="Do shards work in dungeons?">
Most shards drop from overworld mobs. Dungeon runs fill Bestiary entries for the mobs you kill, but shards themselves are primarily from overworld farming.
</Details>
