## How Pets Work

Pets are companions that give passive bonuses while equipped. You can only have **one pet active at a time**. Each pet has:
- A **type** (Combat, Mining, Farming, etc.)
- A **rarity** (Common → Legendary → Mythic)
- A **level** (1–100)
- Optional **Pet Items** (equip one item per pet to boost it)

Pet bonuses scale with **both level and rarity**. A Legendary pet at Level 100 is dramatically stronger than a Rare pet at Level 1.

## Pet Leveling

Pets gain XP automatically as you do the activity they're associated with. To level faster:
- **Use the correct pet for your activity** — a Combat pet gains XP from killing mobs, not farming.
- **Pet XP boost** from accessories and Taming skill increases XP gain.
- **Kat's Workshop** — pay Kat (in Hub) coins + materials to instantly level a pet.

### Kat's Workshop
Kat can upgrade pets two ways:
1. **Level up** — pay coins to jump multiple levels instantly.
2. **Rarity upgrade** — pay coins + items to upgrade e.g. Rare → Epic.

Kat takes time to finish (hours/days in real time). Queue your most valuable pet first.

## Pet Score

**Pet Score** is a meta-progression reward system. You earn score by:
- Leveling pets to Level 100 (each unique pet at 100 gives score)
- Reaching certain milestones

Pet Score rewards include:
- **Magic Find** bonuses
- **Pet Luck** bonuses
- Special cosmetic items

<Info title="Farm Pet Score early">
Even cheap Common/Uncommon pets give Pet Score when maxed. Leveling 10–15 cheap pets to 100 is a worthwhile early investment for the Magic Find bonus.
</Info>

## Taming Skill

The **Taming skill** increases your **Pet Luck** — the chance to find higher-rarity pets and egg drops. It also gives XP multipliers.

| Taming Level | Key Bonus |
|-------------|-----------|
| 1–10 | Small pet luck |
| 25 | +5 Pet Luck |
| 50 | +10 Pet Luck total |

Taming XP comes from any pet being active while you do activities. Some pets (like the Rock) give passive Taming XP.
