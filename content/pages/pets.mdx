## How Pets Work

Pets are companions that give passive bonuses while equipped. Only **one pet can be active** at a time. Each pet has a type (Combat, Mining, Farming, etc.), a rarity, a level (1–100), and an optional **Pet Item** slot.

Pet bonuses scale with **both level and rarity**. A Legendary pet at Level 100 dramatically outperforms a Rare pet at Level 1.

## Pet Leveling

Pets gain XP from the activity matching their type. To level faster:

- **Use the right pet** — a Combat pet gains XP from kills, not farming
- **Taming skill** increases pet XP gain passively
- **Kat's Workshop** — pay coins + materials to instantly level pets

### Kat's Workshop

Kat (in the Hub) can:
1. **Level up** — pay coins to jump multiple levels instantly
2. **Rarity upgrade** — pay coins + items to upgrade e.g. Rare → Epic

Kat takes real time to finish (hours/days). Queue your most valuable pet first so it's working while you play.

## Pet Score

**Pet Score** is a meta-progression system. Earn score by leveling pets to Level 100. Rewards include **Magic Find** and **Pet Luck** bonuses.

<Info title="Farm Pet Score early">
Even cheap Common/Uncommon pets give Pet Score when maxed. Leveling 10–15 cheap pets to Level 100 is a worthwhile early investment for the Magic Find bonus — it helps with every drop in the game.
</Info>

## Taming Skill

The **Taming skill** increases **Pet Luck** — the chance to find higher-rarity pets and better egg drops.

| Taming Level | Key Bonus |
|-------------|-----------|
| 1–10 | Small Pet Luck increase |
| 25 | +5 Pet Luck |
| 50 | +10 Pet Luck total |

Taming XP comes passively from any pet being active during activities. The Rock pet gives passive Taming XP just from being held.
