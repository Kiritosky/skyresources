## How Slayers Work

Slayers are quest-based boss encounters. You:
1. Talk to **Maddox the Slayer** (or slayer NPC in the relevant area) to accept a quest.
2. Kill mobs of the matching type to fill the quest XP bar.
3. When the bar fills, your personal slayer boss spawns — only you (and party) can see and damage it.
4. Kill the boss within the time limit to complete the quest.
5. Completing quests earns **Slayer XP** toward that slayer's level (1–9).

Higher slayer levels unlock better drops, crafting recipes, and permanent stat bonuses.

<Warning title="Only you can damage your boss">
Each player's slayer boss is personal — other players can't steal your boss, but they also can't help kill it (unless you're in the same party). Make sure your gear is strong enough before going up a tier.
</Warning>

## Slayer Level Requirements

Each slayer has 9 levels. XP accumulates across all kills of that type.

## Boss Tiers

Each slayer type has 4 boss tiers (T1–T4). Higher tiers:
- Give more Slayer XP per kill
- Drop rarer items
- Cost more coins to start (the quest fee)
- Require your slayer level to be high enough to unlock

| Tier | Slayer XP Given | Approx. Quest Cost | Req. Slayer Level |
|------|----------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| T1 | 5 | 100–500 coins | None |
| T2 | 25 | 2,000 coins | Level 1 |
| T3 | 100–200 | 10,000 coins | Level 2–3 |
| T4 | 400–500 | 50,000 coins | Level 3–4 |

<Tip title="Run T3 until you can reliably kill T4">
T4 gives 4–5× the XP of T3 but costs ~5× more. If your T4 kill time is very long (3+ min), the T3 rate is often better. Once you one-shot or two-shot T4, switch.
</Tip>

## Recommended Progression Order

1. **Zombie T2–T4** — cheapest, no special gear needed, great starter XP
2. **Spider T1–T3** — drops Tarantula Boots which are essential mid-game combat boots
3. **Wolf T1–T3** — drops Overflux (useful accessory) and Hamster Wheel
4. **Enderman T1–T4** — drops Etherwarp (mobility) and Enderman Sword
5. **Blaze T1–T4** — best in Crimson Isle, requires decent combat gear
6. **Vampire** — unique mechanic (see below)

## Vampire Slayer — Different Mechanics

The Vampire Slayer works differently from the others:
- Vampires are found in the **Vampiric Mine** in the Rift dimension (requires Rift access).
- The boss is the **Riftstalker Bloodfiend**.
- Uses **Motes** (Rift currency) instead of regular coins for the quest fee.
- Drops **Vampire Fang** (accessory) and other Rift-specific items.

<Info title="Vampire requires Rift access">
You must unlock the **Rift** dimension before doing Vampire Slayer. The Rift is a mid-to-late game area unlocked via a special quest chain.
</Info>

## RNG Meter

The **RNG Meter** (unlocked at Slayer Level 3 for each type) lets you trade 10,000 kills worth of RNG into a **guaranteed rare drop**. It fills passively as you do slayer bosses and can be targeted to specific drops.

<Tip title="Set your RNG meter target early">
Decide which rare drop you want before you hit Level 3 and set the target immediately. The meter fills whether or not you have a target set — you can't retroactively claim filled meter for unset targets.
</Tip>
