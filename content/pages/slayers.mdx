## How Slayers Work

Slayers are quest-based boss encounters. You accept a quest from **Maddox the Slayer** (Hub), kill mobs of the matching type to fill a progress bar, then your personal boss spawns and only you (and party members) can see and damage it.

<Steps title="Starting a slayer" steps={[
  { label: "Accept a quest", description: "Talk to Maddox the Slayer in the Hub or the NPC in the relevant area.", status: "done" },
  { label: "Kill mobs", description: "Fill the quest XP bar by killing mobs of the matching type.", status: "done" },
  { label: "Boss spawns", description: "When the bar fills, your personal slayer boss spawns — only visible to you and your party.", status: "current" },
  { label: "Kill the boss", description: "Defeat it within the time limit to complete the quest and earn Slayer XP.", status: "upcoming" }
]} />

Higher slayer levels unlock better drops, crafting recipes, and permanent stat bonuses.

<Warning title="Only you can damage your boss">
Each player's slayer boss is personal — other players cannot steal it, but they also cannot help kill it unless they are in your party. Make sure your gear is strong enough before going up a tier.
</Warning>

Each slayer has 9 levels. XP accumulates across all bosses of that type.

## Boss Tiers

Most slayers have 4 tiers (T1–T4). **Revenant Horror (Zombie) has a T5** — the only slayer with a fifth tier, dropping the rare **Warden Heart**.

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Standard (T1–T4)">

| Tier | Slayer XP Given | Approx. Quest Cost | Req. Slayer Level |
|------|----------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| T1 | 5 | 100–500 coins | None |
| T2 | 25 | 2,000 coins | Level 1 |
| T3 | 100 | 10,000 coins | Level 2–3 |
| T4 | 400 | 50,000 coins | Level 3–4 |

</Tab>
<Tab label="Revenant T5">

Revenant Horror T5 is the hardest and most expensive slayer encounter.

| Tier | Slayer XP | Quest Cost | Req. Level | Key Drop |
|------|-----------|------------|------------|---------|
| T5 | 600 | 100,000 coins | Level 7 | Warden Heart |

The **Warden Heart** is used to craft the **Warden Helmet** — a powerful armor piece for endgame zombie/undead combat.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip title="Run T3 until you can reliably kill T4">
T4 gives more XP but costs 5× more. If your T4 kill time exceeds 3 minutes, T3 is usually better XP/hr. Switch once you can one-shot or two-shot T4.
</Tip>

## Vampire Slayer — Different Mechanics

The Vampire Slayer works differently from the others:

- Found in the **Vampiric Mine** inside the **Rift** dimension (requires Rift access)
- Uses **Motes** (Rift currency) instead of coins for quest fees
- Boss regenerates health — use **Garlic** and **Wooden Stake** items to counter it
- Drops **Vampire Fang** and other Rift-specific accessories

<Info title="Vampire requires Rift access">
Unlock the Rift dimension first via the quest chain with Elise in the Hub. The Rift is a mid-to-late game area and has a separate inventory system.
</Info>

## RNG Meter

The **RNG Meter** (unlocked at Slayer Level 3 for each type) lets you trade 10,000 kills worth of accumulated RNG into a **guaranteed rare drop** of your choice.

<Details title="How does the RNG Meter work?">
When you reach Slayer Level 3, an RNG Meter appears in your Slayer menu. It fills passively as you do boss kills. Once full, you can exchange it for one guaranteed rare drop from a list for that slayer type. You must **set a target** before it fills — unset meters can't be retroactively claimed.
</Details>

<Tip title="Set your RNG meter target immediately">
The meter fills whether or not a target is set. Set it the moment you unlock it at Level 3 so you don't waste any accumulated progress.
</Tip>
