## You're in Mid Game If…

- You have **Skill Average 20–35**
- You can complete **Dungeons Floor 4–5**
- You have at least **100 Magical Power** from accessories
- You're earning **1–5M coins/hr** consistently

Mid game is about **building your Magical Power**, pushing dungeons to Floor 6, and picking a money-making lane to fund endgame gear.

---

## The Magical Power Wall

**Magical Power (MP)** is the single most important mid-game stat. It comes entirely from accessories (talismans, rings, orbs, etc.) in your Accessory Bag. MP scales your combat stats logarithmically — every point helps, with the biggest relative gains at the start.

<TableCard title="MP by Accessory Rarity">

| Rarity | MP per piece |
|--------|-------------|
| Common | 3 |
| Uncommon | 5 |
| Rare | 8 |
| Epic | 12 |
| Legendary | 16 |
| Mythic | 22 |

</TableCard>

<Tip title="Hit 300 MP before buying expensive single upgrades">
The 0–300 MP range gives the best stat gain per coin spent. Fill your bag with accessible accessories before chasing rare or expensive individual upgrades.
</Tip>

## Building Magical Power Efficiently

<Checklist title="MP optimization checklist">
- Check your current accessories on SkyCrypt → Accessories section
- Remove all duplicates — only the highest rarity of each family counts
- Fill empty bag slots with any accessible accessories
- Reforge accessories to Warped for general use
- Equip the right Power Stone for your activity
- Upgrade the highest-rarity pieces you have with Recombobulator 3000
- Use tuning points — don't leave them unspent
</Checklist>

## Key Milestones to Hit

<Checklist title="Mid game progression checklist">
- Unlock Dwarven Mines (give Rhys 15 enchanted ores)
- Reach Heart of the Mountain Level 5
- Clear Dungeons Floor 5 for the first time
- Reach Slayer Level 5 in at least one type
- Hit 300 Magical Power
- Unlock your Garden (Farming Islands quest)
- Get a Legendary pet at Level 100
</Checklist>
