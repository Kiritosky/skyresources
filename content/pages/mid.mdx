## You're in Mid Game If…

- You have **Skill Average 20–35**
- You can do **Dungeons Floor 4–5**
- You have at least **100 Magic Power** from accessories
- You're earning **1–5M coins/hr** consistently

Mid game is about **building your Magic Power**, pushing dungeons to Floor 6, and picking a money-making lane to fund endgame gear.

---

## The Magic Power Wall

**Magic Power (MP)** is the single most important mid-game stat. It comes entirely from accessories (talismans, rings, orbs, etc.) equipped in your Accessory Bag.

Every accessory rarity gives different MP:
| Rarity | MP per piece |
|--------|-------------|
| Common | 3 |
| Uncommon | 5 |
| Rare | 8 |
| Epic | 12 |
| Legendary | 16 |
| Mythic | 22 |
| Special | 3 |
| Very Special | 5 |

<Tip title="MP breakpoints matter">
MP unlocks bonus stats at every 10-point milestone. At **300 MP** you unlock Strength/Crit scaling. At **500 MP** you unlock a major damage multiplier. Prioritize reaching 300, then 500.
</Tip>

## Dungeons Progression

Dungeons are the main mid-game content. Push floors in order — each unlock gives new gear.

| Floor | Catacombs Req. | Key Drop |
|-------|----------------|---------|
| F1 | None | Basic loot |
| F2 | None | Bonzo's Staff / Scarf gear |
| F3 | None | Adaptive Armor |
| F4 | Cata 12 | Shadow Assassin |
| F5 | Cata 18 | Livid Dagger |
| F6 | Cata 24 | Necron / Maxor / Storm gear |
| F7 | Cata 28 | Wither Blade / Hyperion |

<Warning title="Class levels matter">
Each dungeon class (Archer, Mage, Berserk, Healer, Tank) has its own level. Your class stats scale with your class level. Running your main class consistently is faster than rotating.
</Warning>

## Slayer Progression

Slayers are key mid-game content. They unlock gear, drop valuable items, and give Combat XP.

**Recommended progression order:**
1. **Zombie** (Revenant) — cheapest, easiest to start
2. **Spider** (Tarantula) — drops Tarantula Boots, essential for combat
3. **Wolf** (Sven) — drops Overflux, useful utility
4. **Enderman** (Voidgloom) — drops Etherwarp / Enderman Sword
5. **Blaze** (Inferno) — Crimson Isle content, hard but rewarding
6. **Vampire** — requires defeating previous slayers first

<Info title="Slayer cost vs. profit">
Slayer quests cost coins to start. Always check if your expected drops + XP value exceeds the startup cost. At T4, most slayers are profitable even without rare drops.
</Info>

## Building Magic Power (MP) Efficiently

1. **Check your current accessories** on SkyCrypt → `/skills` tab → Accessories section.
2. **Remove duplicates** — each unique accessory type counts once only.
3. **Upgrade rarities** — a Rare Talisman gives more MP than a Common one (recombobulate or upgrade).
4. **Reforge** — most accessories benefit from the **Warped** reforge for MP efficiency.
5. **Power Stone** — use a **Power Stone** that matches your playstyle (e.g., Sorrow for combat).

- [ ] Unlock **Dwarven Mines** (give Rhys 15 enchanted ores)

- [ ] Clear **Dungeons Floor 5** for the first time
- [ ] Reach **Slayer Level 5** in at least one type

- [ ] Unlock your **Garden** (Farming Islands quest)
