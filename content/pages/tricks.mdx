## Quality of Life

Small habits compound into big time savings. These tips help you play faster and smarter.

### Navigation Shortcuts
- `/warp [location]` — teleport instantly (e.g. `/warp hub`, `/warp garden`)
- `/sethome` + `/home` — set a private island teleport
- `/ah` — open Auction House directly

## Economy Tips

### Checking Prices
Always check **two sources** before buying or selling:
1. **Bazaar** — for stackable items (sell instantly or set buy orders)
2. **Auction House** — for unique items (search by item name)

Use [COFL](https://sky.coflnet.com/) for price history. A price spike or drop in the last 24 hours changes the right decision.

<Tip title="Buy orders save millions">
Instead of instant-buying from the Bazaar (which uses the sell price, usually higher), place a **Buy Order** slightly below the listed buy price. You often fill within minutes and save 5–15%.
</Tip>

### Compound Your Coins
- Don't let coins sit idle. Put them in **Minions**, **Accessory upgrades**, or **Pets** that generate returns.
- Reinvesting 80% of your income is how players snowball their net worth.

## Farming Tips

- **Never leave mid-harvest** — incomplete harvests waste time and XP.
- **Telekinesis on your hoe** — auto-picks up crops, no clicking needed.
- **Replenish enchant** — auto-replants crops, massively increases speed.
- **Check Farming Fortune** on SkyCrypt before buying new gear — know your baseline.
- **Stay in the Garden** — it gives 3× Farming XP vs. your Private Island.

## Dungeon Tips

- **Take notes on room layouts** — after 50+ runs you'll memorize secrets.
- **Never skip crypts** — killing 6+ crypts in F6/F7 gives a big score bonus.
- **Communicate puzzle assignments** before entering — speeds up every run.
- **S+ score = better chest roll** — focus on full exploration + low deaths.
- **Use SkyHanni** — it shows secret locations and puzzle solutions automatically.

<Warning title="Deaths tank your score">
Each death reduces your score by a significant amount. A single death on F7 can drop you from S+ to S. Prioritize survivability over raw damage at new floors.
</Warning>

## Mining Tips

- **Powder routes** — take Powder whenever it spawns (glowing blocks). Never skip it.
- **Keep drills fueled** — an empty drill is worthless. Keep spare fuel in storage.
- **Progress HotM first** — without HotM 7+, many setups are not worth investing in.
- **Use the Party Finder** for Crystal Hollows — full parties find nodes faster.

---

## Slayer Tips

- **Check kill time before upgrading a tier** — if T4 takes 3+ min, T3 is usually better XP/hr.
- **Set your RNG meter target immediately at Level 3** — the meter fills whether or not you have a target.
- **Bring the right pet** — a Combat pet at Level 100 dramatically speeds up kill times.
- **Check quest cost vs. expected drops** — every tier should be profitable on average.

## Time Management

SkyBlock has daily/hourly resets that you should build a routine around:

| Reset | What Resets |
|-------|------------|
| 20 SkyBlock minutes (~20 real min) | Experimentation Table, some quests |
| Daily (midnight EST) | Daily quests, NPC shops |
| Election cycle (~5 days) | Mayor changes |
| Seasonal | Jacob's Contest schedule, seasonal events |

<Info title="Do Experimentation Table every day">
The Experimentation Table resets every 20 SkyBlock minutes. At high Enchanting levels, running it daily gives millions of Enchanting XP for 2–3 minutes of work. It's one of the highest XP/minute activities.
</Info>

## Avoiding Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Buying gear before checking requirements | Always check the item's requirements first |
| Ignoring Fairy Souls | Collect them — free permanent stats |
| Switching methods every patch | Pick one, optimize it for 2–4 weeks minimum |
| Not using Buy Orders on Bazaar | Place orders — saves 5–15% vs instant buy |
| Leaving minions full for days | Check minions daily — full = no income |
| Forgetting to pet swap | Use SkyHanni auto-swap feature |
| Buying at hype peak | Wait 24–48h after a patch before buying "new meta" items |
