## What Are Skills?

Skills are core progression systems in SkyBlock. You gain XP in each skill by performing related activities. Higher skill levels give permanent stat bonuses and unlock new items, recipes, and features.

There are **12 skills** total: Farming, Mining, Combat, Foraging, Fishing, Enchanting, Alchemy, Taming, Carpentry, Runecrafting, Social, and Dungeons.

<Info title="Skill Average">
Your **Skill Average** is the average level of your 8 core skills (excludes Carpentry, Runecrafting, Social). It is shown on SkyCrypt and affects your networth display.
</Info>

## Farming XP Guide

Farming XP is earned by harvesting crops. Use a **Mooshroom Cow pet** or **Elephant pet** to multiply XP. The Garden (unlocked through the Farming Islands) massively boosts your farming speed and XP.

| Crop | XP per action | Notes |
|------|---------------|-------|
| Pumpkin | 3.0 | Very fast 2-block harvest, great with Fortune |
| Sugar Cane | 3.0 per block | Requires Replenish enchant on hoe |
| Wheat | 2.4 | Easiest to set up |
| Carrot / Potato | 3.0 | Good with Mooshroom Cow |
| Melon | 2.4 | Good for early Farming |
| Nether Wart | 5.0 | Excellent XP, unlock in Crimson Isle |
| Cocoa Beans | 2.0 | Fast if set up properly |

<Warning title="Garden vs Hub Farm">
Farming in the **Garden** gives a 3× XP multiplier over normal island farming. Always farm in your Garden once you unlock it.
</Warning>

## Mining XP Guide

Mining XP comes from breaking ores and stone in mining zones. The **Dwarven Mines** and **Crystal Hollows** are the main endgame spots.

| Location | Ore | Notes |
|----------|-----|-------|
| Gold Mine | Gold | Early game only |
| Deep Caverns | Iron/Gold/Diamond | Pre-Dwarven |
| Dwarven Mines | Mithril | Best early-mid XP, also gives Powder |
| Crystal Hollows | Gemstones | Best mid-late XP |
| Mineshaft (HOTM 10) | Ores | Best late game |

## Combat XP Guide

Combat XP is gained by killing mobs. The more HP the mob has, the more XP it gives.

**Best methods by stage:**
- **Early**: Hub Spiders, Crimson Isle mobs
- **Mid**: Dungeons (Floor 4–6), Enderman Slayer
- **Late**: Dungeons M3–M6, Vampire Slayer

---

## Fishing XP Guide

Fishing XP is earned by catching fish and sea creatures. Use a **Dolphin pet** for XP multiplier early on, or a **Whale pet** at higher Fishing levels.

- Fish in the **Frozen Ocean** for early XP
- **Lava Fishing** in the Crimson Isle gives the best XP per hour once you have the right rod
- Trophy Fishing (Carnival) gives XP but focuses on rare trophy fish

---

## Enchanting XP Guide

Enchanting XP comes from using the **Enchanting Table**, the **Experimentation Table**, and combining/applying enchantments on the anvil.

<Tip title="Fastest Enchanting XP">
The **Experimentation Table** minigames give a ton of XP per day. Do them every day — they reset at midnight server time.
</Tip>

| Source | XP | Notes |
|--------|-----|-------|
| Enchanting table (books) | Low | Passive, always happening |
| Experimentation Table | Very High | Do daily — best per-time method |
| Combining enchant books | Medium | Each combine gives XP |
| Enchanting gear | Medium | Applies to gear enchants too |

---

## Alchemy XP Guide

Alchemy XP is earned by brewing potions. The fastest method is looping **Water Bottles → Speed Potions** (or any cheap potion). Ingredients are cheap since you only care about the XP, not the output.

<Warning title="Ingredient Cost">
Alchemy XP farming costs ingredients. Set aside a budget (1–2M coins) before grinding it. Speed/Strength potions are cheapest to loop.
</Warning>

---

## Taming XP Guide

Taming XP comes from equipping and playing with pets. Certain pets passively give Taming XP just by being active. The **Rock pet** gives Taming XP just from being held.
