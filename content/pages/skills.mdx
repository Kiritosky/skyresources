## What Are Skills?

Skills are core progression systems in SkyBlock. You gain XP by performing related activities. Higher skill levels give permanent stat bonuses and unlock new items, recipes, and features.

There are **12 skills** total: Farming, Mining, Combat, Foraging, Fishing, Enchanting, Alchemy, Taming, Carpentry, Runecrafting, Social, and Dungeons (Catacombs).

<Info title="Skill Average">
Your **Skill Average** is the average level of your 8 core skills (excludes Carpentry, Runecrafting, Social, Dungeons). It displays on SkyCrypt and affects your networth and stat display.
</Info>

## Farming XP Guide

Use a **Mooshroom Cow** or **Elephant pet** to multiply XP. The Garden (unlocked through the Farming Islands quest) gives 3× Farming XP vs. your Private Island.

<TableCard title="Crop XP Rates">

| Crop | XP per harvest | Notes |
|------|----------------|-------|
| Pumpkin | 3.0 | Fast 2-block harvest, great with Fortune |
| Sugar Cane | 3.0 per block | Requires Replenish enchant on hoe |
| Wheat | 2.4 | Easiest to set up |
| Carrot / Potato | 3.0 | Good with Mooshroom Cow |
| Melon | 2.4 | Good Bazaar price |
| Nether Wart | 5.0 | Excellent XP, requires Crimson Isle access |
| Cocoa Beans | 2.0 | Fast if set up correctly |

</TableCard>

<Warning title="Garden vs Hub Farm">
Farming in the **Garden** gives a **3× XP multiplier** over normal island farming. Always farm in your Garden once you unlock it.
</Warning>

## Enchanting XP Guide

Enchanting XP comes from the **Enchanting Table**, **Experimentation Table**, and combining books on the anvil.

<Tip title="Fastest Enchanting XP">
The **Experimentation Table** resets every 20 SkyBlock minutes (~20 real minutes). At Enchanting 60 it gives millions of XP per reset. Run it every reset — 2–3 minutes of work.
</Tip>

| Source | XP | Notes |
|--------|-----|-------|
| Experimentation Table | Very High | Best time-efficiency — do every reset |
| Combining enchant books | Medium | Each combine gives XP |
| Enchanting gear | Medium | Applies to gear and book enchants |
| Enchanting table (books) | Low | Passive, always happening |

---

## Alchemy XP Guide

Alchemy XP is earned by brewing potions. The fastest method loops **Water Bottles → Speed or Strength Potions** — you only care about the XP, not the output.

<Warning title="Ingredient cost">
Set aside a budget of 1–2M coins before grinding Alchemy. Speed/Strength potions are the cheapest ingredients for XP loops.
</Warning>

---

## Taming XP Guide

Taming XP comes from any pet being active while you do activities. Some pets (like the **Rock pet**) give Taming XP passively just by being held in your inventory.
